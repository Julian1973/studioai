## What was fixed (all verified against the real Box Monsters data; suite 992 passed, 0 failed)

**The canon re-lock stranded the episode** (G6, G5, G1). Scenes 3/5 were blocked behind "Run
Story & Direction" — the destructive path, which would have cascaded into scene 1/2's approved
SEE/HEAR/WATCH ledgers — while `rebase_canon_lock` passed every precondition. Re-locking canon
now rebases the beat package, the vision, approved AND awaiting storyboards, and the production
packages' provenance; `intake_status` names the reason (`canonicalReason`, `rebaseEligible`); the
desk offers a zero-spend "Carry Story & Direction onto the new canon lock" action.

**The desk never dead-ends** (G2, G10, G3, G4). Keyframe readiness is cb_state's currency
verdict, never the asset registry's raw flag (scene 2's stale approvals were routed past SEE into
a guaranteed WATCH refusal). A stale SEE approval offers rebuild/re-confirm; a stale HEAR approval
offers rebuild; a stale accepted take offers reopen; model-limited offers override with a
redesign note; a batch left "generating" with no job offers abandon (which now releases the
sqlite claims). WATCH refuses a stale SEE anchor. `build_keyframe` re-prepares stale direction.
Scene-wide job failures show even with a shot selected.

**Voice** (G3 F1 = G7 F3 = G10 F8). This morning's `7089453e` compared the approval against a
differently shaped signature, so any Regenerate superseded a CURRENT approval with a fabricated
reason. Fixed to `voice_approval_status`. `_SOUND` no longer deletes ordinary words (laugh,
sneeze…) from the ElevenLabs text; the per-line TTS fallback is off by default and loud when on.

**The engine held the first show** (G7 F2, rule 87). `engine/grammar_pack.json` carried the

vocabulary into every Box Monsters keyframe and animation prompt; the project's `laws/style.txt`
never reached the provider. Those keys now live in `projects/<id>/laws/director_grammar.json`
(profile `laws.directorGrammar`). Consequence: scene 1's two pending takes now read "rendered from
a superseded direction" — correct, their prompts carried the wrong look; the desk offers
Reject-and-re-seal (one render each).

**Departments read their whole brief** (G7 F1). `_j` truncated every worker's context at 22,000
characters; real shot contexts are 25–58k — the ordered attachments, the human WATCH feedback and

**Post keeps the accepted take** (G4). The provider mix is the deliverable (no post voice swap,

## Decisions only Julian can make

Ruled the same evening: (1) stay at 480p and upscale downstream; (2) the voice audio is always
supplied — Law 6 means no synthesised speech, the transcript is the lip-sync binding; (3) the
Teacher is a one-off, no reference asset needed; (5) SEE and HEAR keep firing directly behind the
billing confirmation — no disclosure step (Julian: "let's leave them"). Item 4 remains open.

1. **Delivery resolution.** The default route verifies 480p only (854×480). Qualify 720p/1080p on
   BytePlus, or route finals through fal at 720p (~4.5× the per-take cost).
2. **Law 6 wording.** Video prompts carry the exact lines as a braced transcript bound to
   @Audio1 and the fire gate REQUIRES them (lip-sync evidence, never synthesis). The law's text
   says "no spoken words". Amend the wording or the emission standard.
3. **The Teacher.** S1.SH01's direction has her walking into frame with no locked identity
   reference; every take invents her. She needs a reference asset locked into canon.
4. **fal as the alternate route** stays enabled, selectable by `CB_VIDEO_MODEL_ID`. Keep or disable.
5. **Image and voice spend** (SEE ×2 images, HEAR auditions + track) fire behind a billing
   confirmation but no sealed disclosure token, unlike WATCH.
