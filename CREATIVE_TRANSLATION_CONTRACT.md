# Creative Translation Contract

This contract governs the existing path from approved script to approved animation prompt.
It does not create another pipeline, approval gate, provider route or user-interface step.

## Authority

1. The immutable script owns events and dialogue order.
2. Story & Direction owns the joke or ache, status change, audience progression and heart.
3. The approved beat contracts own emotional and comic structure.
4. The approved storyboard owns production-unit boundaries, causal stages and motivated views.
5. The approved performance contract owns observable character acting and physical causality.
6. Cinematography owns the performance-ready stage, camera grammar, light and composition.
7. Voice owns the final ElevenLabs performance. Spoken words reach Seedance through `@Audio1`,
   never through provider prompt text.
8. Animation compiles these approved decisions into Seedance grammar without weakening them.
9. Julian approves the visible references, direction and exact prompt before any paid fire.

## Required Animation Translation

Every Animation Director candidate contains:

- a director interpretation naming the joke or ache, mechanism, status before and after,
  three audience reads and emotional heart;
- one gag clock for every approved `SMALL` or `BIG` comedy beat, in source order;
- directly visible anticipation and reaction around each approved disruption;
- a recovery hold of no more than 1.5 seconds;
- one dialogue-free `providerAction` sentence for each gag, copied verbatim into the final
  provider prompt;
- a generation-design record explaining the approved unit density and copying the approved
  final handoff state exactly.

The approved setup, disruption, hold and button are copied into the typed gag clock without
rewriting. Only observable action is compiled into provider prose. Analysis language and
spoken dialogue remain outside the provider prompt.

## Deterministic Refusal

Before spend, the engine refuses when:

- an approved stage event or end state is missing, changed or reordered;
- an approved comedy beat has no matching gag clock;
- a gag clock changes the approved setup, impact, hold or button;
- its dialogue-free `providerAction` is absent from the exact provider prompt;
- spoken words leak into a `providerAction` or provider prompt;
- the approved final handoff state changes;
- reference, audio, provider capability, billing or spend requirements are incomplete.

Creative quality still requires human judgment after rendering. Deterministic checks can prove
that direction survived compilation; they cannot prove that a probabilistic model performed it
well.

## Iteration

Review the rendered media before changing direction. Record what landed and diagnose the failing
layer: direction, keyframe, reference, voice, take or post. Preserve successful elements and
change only the failing contract. No daily-render rule and no compulsory candidate count may
override explicit spend approval.
