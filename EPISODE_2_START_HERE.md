# Start Here — an episode in any production

StudioAI: http://127.0.0.1:8899/cb-studio/app.html (or the launch URL `start-studio.cmd` prints)

On the **Productions** screen select the show (Crystal Bears, The Box Monsters, or one you create with
**New production**), choose the episode, and open **Script**. For Crystal Bears Episode 2 the approved
script is `Ep2_Bo_s_Big_Day_V2.txt`; for The Box Monsters Episode 1 it is
`Ep1_Episode_1_The_Box_That_Felt_Small.txt` — review it and approve the script.

The four phases are:

1. Script
2. Production Package
3. Render
4. Review

Julian's decisions are: approve the script, press **Fire**, and choose **Approve**, **Retake**
or **Reject** after review.

After script approval, StudioAI prepares the package automatically. Open the scene to see the
story and beats, shot plan, keyframe, ElevenLabs v3 voice bed and waveform, Seedance 2.5 prompt,
references, continuity, timing budget, score, warnings and cost. Fire stays blocked until the
package is complete and the required approval is present.

To stop safely, use **STOP** in StudioAI. To resume, reopen the address above; completed ledger
work remains recorded.

**First action:** open StudioAI, select the production and its episode, open Script, and review the
uploaded script. Every production has its own canon, laws, chairs' taste, assets and episodes under
`projects/<id>/`; nothing of one show is visible from another.
