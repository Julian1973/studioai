  gags, invalid crystal bearers, lost dialogue occurrences or incomplete performance contracts.

  contract, model ID, request schema and price are verified.

  upload; already-uploaded references are not uploaded twice.
- Scene post now builds real 16:9 and 9:16 masters, exact captions and 24-bit programme audio at

  duration before exposing a candidate for human final review.
- A validated show profile isolates tenant paths and names the installed creative adapter.
  Another IP cannot fall through to Crystal Bears content or use this adapter by accident.
- The loopback server remains launch-authenticated and same-origin. It now also caps request and
  document sizes and verifies uploaded image bytes, type and dimensions.
