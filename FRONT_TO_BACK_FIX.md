# 8Th Hour — front-to-back production fix

This update establishes one production path:

1. approve Story & Direction;
2. approve Look Development direction;
3. generate or select and approve the Scene Look;
4. approve Cinematography, create and approve the opening frame;
5. approve Voice direction, generate, listen to and approve the take;
6. approve Animation direction;
7. review the exact prompt, references, audio and cost before generation;
8. approve one animation candidate;
9. carry its harvested final frame into the next shot;
10. stitch and review the finished scene.

The Production readiness panel reports every known blocker together. Paid actions accept
only current approved specialist outputs; legacy prompts and unapproved working text cannot
silently reach a provider.

To install, double-click `INSTALL_UPDATE.command`. It updates source code only and preserves
the existing `.env`, media, approvals, output and production history.
