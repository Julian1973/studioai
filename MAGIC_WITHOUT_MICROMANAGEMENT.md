# Magic Without Micromanagement

## Governing principle

The pipeline protects the dramatic truth and gives Seedance room to perform.
It directs an exceptional actor and camera crew; it does not prescribe animation
frame by frame.

## What is locked

- exact script event and approved dialogue/audio;
- character, world, scale and prop canon;
- reference roles and opening continuity;
- who owns the beat;
- the audience feeling before and after the turn;
- the essential cause, payoff and closing handoff.

## What remains creatively open

- acting cadence and micro-reactions;
- thought processes expressed through eyes, breath, posture and recovery;
- overlap, follow-through and secondary motion;
- natural variation inside the approved performance arc;
- organic camera response that strengthens the beat;
- richer comic or emotional detail that preserves the story truth.

## Direction density

- **Open** — default for performance-led material. State the objective, relationship,
  obstacle, turn and landing; let Seedance discover the detailed performance.
- **Guided** — use when action geography, escalation or a particular reveal needs clearer
  staging.
- **Precise** — use only when exact continuity, dialogue synchronisation, safety or an
  essential physical event requires it. The reason must be recorded.

## Evaluation

A take is judged by what the audience actually feels, not by literal obedience to every
suggested gesture. An unexpected choice is a success when it preserves canon and story,
strengthens the intended beat and improves the scene.

The Director Agent diagnoses weak outcomes without automatically adding more instructions.
It first considers whether the problem was the opening frame, reference binding, overloaded
action, timing, camera plan, audio, provider variance or the edit. If another render is
needed, it changes one evidenced lever and preserves everything that already worked.
