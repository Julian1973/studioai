# Current production contract · 7 September 2026

**You direct the film instead of managing the machinery.**

## Authority

The current script and locked show canon govern story and identity. The shared
[production standard](skills/production-standard.md) and marked runtime department skills
govern craft preparation. `engine/cb_departments.py` compiles typed direction; selected
provider capability records govern transport and limits. Dated audits and `docs/archive/`
explain history and must not supply runtime instructions.

`cb_state.py` derives production readiness; `cb_production_contracts.py` owns shared graph
and acceptance rules. `cb_render.py` composes policy and transaction boundaries explicitly.
Accepted media is distinct from the readiness of a new draft.

## Episode and human decisions

1. Upload the script. Review and approve one editable, provisional episode allowance.
2. The Studio prepares interpretation, episode/scene direction and technical briefs internally.
3. **SEE:** review and approve the opening keyframe. Select explicitly when A/B candidates exist.
4. **HEAR:** listen and approve the exact voice performance. Skip this decision for silent shots.
5. **WATCH request:** read the exact animation prompt, references and script; approve submission.
6. **WATCH result:** watch the returned candidate and approve or request a correction.
7. Continue to the next shot using the accepted predecessor's continuity. Review the scene cut
   and final master when those assembled results are ready.

Each join has an authored editorial choice: continuation uses the accepted landing frame;
a planned cut (including reverse coverage) receives its own SEE keyframe. Cuts carry the
preceding world and action state while changing camera composition. The declared cut
controls frame sourcing even if an older keyframe flag disagrees. Both generation prompts
receive the join instruction, and newly prepared shots show the choice in the shot strip.
This uses the existing approvals; it does not add another review stage.

Internal preparation is attributed to Studio Director. It never manufactures Julian's media
approval. Budget approval grants an allowance, not artistic approval. One WATCH candidate is
the default; the existing explicit SEE A/B comparison remains a separate visual choice.

## Director desk

A persistent scene and shot strip sits above the media review. The Director conversation
stays beside the selected shot on desktop and follows the media on smaller screens.
SEE, HEAR and WATCH open their review surfaces directly; generation prerequisites never
redirect a bookmarked review into an internal department.

`cb_production_contracts.shot_next_action` supplies the next step to the desk, chat and
navigation. “Continue” prepares that step within the approved allowance or opens an existing
review. It cannot approve an artifact or submit a sealed WATCH request. The visible media
approval buttons and chat decisions use the same hash-bound outcome command and automatic
next-stage preparation. Explicit candidate selection remains required for multiple renders.

A correction preview names the affected stages and explains continuity consequences.
Technical inspection and additional review tools remain available through disclosures.

## Change, recovery and learning

Bind each decision to the exact reviewed artifact or sealed request. Recheck it under the
scene lease before applying it. Protect exact dialogue, identity, approved audio, required
story events, reference integrity, provider limits and authorized spending.

Revise the affected inputs. Preserve other accepted work; show downstream continuity
consequences rather than silently rewriting it. Relevant review observations inform future
direction as advisory evidence, never as new canon or universal rules. Craft scores advise;
they cannot grant artistic approval or justify arbitrary repeat loops.

Reserve configured estimated provider costs atomically before submission. Estimates are USD
excluding tax and do not guarantee invoices. Existing spend survives script changes and
allowance updates. Resume known BytePlus tasks by ID. Reconcile an unknown submission before
another paid attempt; do not claim that a missing response means no charge.
