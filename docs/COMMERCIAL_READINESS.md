# Studio AI commercial readiness

## Defensible positioning

Studio AI is an internally developed, human-directed production software platform
which integrates third-party AI services. Its own production orchestration,
approval controls, continuity records, project data and interface are the proposed
proprietary layer. Do not claim ownership of the underlying foundation models.
Ownership of every contribution and asset has not been certified by this review.

The current release is a local workstation candidate, with a qualified existing
animation workflow and additional-project script development. It is not yet a
qualified general-purpose live-action platform or shared multi-user studio system.

## Evidence needed for the proprietary claim

The inspected GitHub repository is public. No root licence was found. Public
visibility is not proof of open-source licensing, but it means the source is
visible and can be forked through GitHub. A private repository does not revoke
copies already made. Confirm the confidentiality policy before further release.

Confirm the legal entity that owns the original code, employee/contractor
assignments, source provenance, AI-assisted contribution terms, asset rights and
third-party dependencies. Retain dependency notices. A proprietary licence must
cover only material the company has the rights to license; do not label third-party
libraries or provider models as company-owned.

## Route to revenue

Start with paid production services using the internal studio and a defined
deliverable. Quote creative work, generation budget, edit/finishing and revision
limits separately. Show actual sample outputs and measured costs.

Next, offer a bounded design-partner pilot: one client, one production type, one
workflow, agreed acceptance criteria and operator support. Do not promise every
genre, guaranteed generation quality or unattended autonomous filmmaking.

Software licensing follows demonstrated installation, recovery, support terms,
rights clearance and repeatable customer results. Define licence scope, model/API
charges, operator training, updates and ownership of customer production assets.

## Pitch evidence

Show the actual interface and a full script-to-export production. Record operator
time, provider spend, rejected takes, manual corrections and finished deliverables.
Present test results as engineering evidence, not as proof of commercial or
artistic readiness. Investor/partner messaging should distinguish the existing
workflow, current onboarding and the general-platform roadmap.
