# Emotional Story Architecture

This is the walk-forward episode layer above the existing v4 scene-directing standard. It
applies when Story & Direction authors a new episode vision. Existing approved episode and
scene packages remain valid and are not silently migrated.

## Episode Contract

New episode direction must establish:

- an action-based story truth: practical want, limiting belief, key relationship, fear,
  transformed action and theme proved through behaviour;
- exactly seven ordered movements: opening, inciting pressure, first adaptation, midpoint
  truth, low point, climax choice and new normal;
- a restrained tapestry across physical and visual motifs, colour and light, source sound,
  music and environmental pressure; and
- a sequence blueprint that records objective, emotional start, pressure, turn, changed end
  condition, dominant audience feeling and the question carried forward.

The scene heart must operate inside that architecture. It may deepen a movement but cannot
resolve the episode early, contradict the sequence objective or detach a motif from its
episode trajectory.

## Shot Contract

Every newly drafted shot states what the audience must understand, what must remain unknown,
the reaction beat, relationship distance and power, touch or avoidance, eyeline logic,
silhouette read, silence rule and score instruction. Review checks the result muted and in
silhouette as well as with final sound.

These fields make emotional causality visible to departments. They are directing evidence,
not an automatic artistic score.

## Authority Boundaries

- Locked screenplay dialogue and signed canon remain authoritative.
- Character-specific behaviour takes precedence over generic camera recipes.
- Music is withheld or used with intent; it does not manufacture emotion missing on screen.
- No model may submit a provider job, authorize spend, approve media or advance a production
  gate. Human approval remains the authority at Story & Direction, SEE, HEAR and WATCH.
