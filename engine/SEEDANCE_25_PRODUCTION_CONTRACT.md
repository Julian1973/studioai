# Seedance production contract

Use [the current Studio contract](../THE_DEFINITIVE_PIPELINE.md) and
[provider evidence and qualification](../skills/provider-evidence.md).

The executable model, endpoint and limits are resolved by `cb_providers.request_contract()`.
Do not infer settings from an old model name, another provider's endpoint, or an announcement.
The current production adapter uses the configured BytePlus ModelArk route. One candidate
is the default; the director authorizes the exact sealed request and bounded cost.

Creative duration, exact dialogue, approved audio and continuity determine the shot.
Craft scores and suggested stage/view counts advise; typed structure and actual route limits
are enforced. The compiler owns reference numbering and exact transcript placement.

The [earlier fal route document](../docs/archive/2026-09-07-pre-consolidation/SEEDANCE_25_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md)
is preserved for historical attribution and is not an executable instruction.
