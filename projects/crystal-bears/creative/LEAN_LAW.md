# THE LAW

Eight rules. Every one was paid for with a real fire and a real verdict — nothing here is
reasoned ahead of evidence. The worked exemplar attached to this law does the rest of the
teaching; these are only the things an exemplar cannot show you.

1. **Audio.** If anyone speaks, open with: *ENGLISH DIALOGUE ONLY, spoken in English. Use
   @Audio1 as the sole source of dialogue, wording, voice, performance and timing.* Name
   who speaks and who stays closed-mouthed. **Never write the words themselves.**

2. **References, one job each.** @图1 for the opening state only; one image per character
   for identity only; one for the world only. Say "only" and mean it.

3. **Never describe how a character looks.** Not once. The references carry appearance;
   your words carry what they DO.

4. **One physical cause, two or three visible consequences.** Weight shows in landing
   compression, momentum in overshoot and recovery. Never a checklist of verbs — the model
   executes causes and butchers adjectives.

5. **Stasis verbs are commands — and stillness is written as a task, never as a state.**
   settle, hold, anchor, steady, still, two-shot: the model obeys them literally. Measured
   at matched length, the approved keeper carries 1 stop/park phrase per 722 words and the
   two rejects carry 9 and 10 — that density, not word count, is what separates the corpus.
   A character who is not moving gets a continuous named task plus micro-corrections running
   the whole shot, and the deadpan lives on the face. "flat", "near-motionless", "barely
   moves" are freeze commands.
   *(Corrected 2026-07-26: this rule used to licence these verbs "where stillness is the
   point". In both rejections the stillness WAS the point — Zenny's deadpan — and describing
   it as a state is exactly what killed them.)*

6. **One geography per character — an anchor that moves with them.** Weld them to a named
   object and keep their place relative to it; when the object travels, they travel with it.
   The anchor is the geography; an absolute position never is. What must not co-exist is a
   fixed position and a contradicting move — that resolves to less motion, every time.
   *(Corrected 2026-07-26: this rule said "Held to a named object, or travelling. Never
   both", which the best prompt in the corpus breaks in a single sentence — "Whenever her
   flower moves she travels with it, keeping her place relative to its centre". The moving
   anchor is what actually stopped the teleporting; the either/or reading refuses it.)*

7. **The card decides how it ends.** Hold, carry, cut on action, or transition. There is no
   default ending.

8. **Spend words only on what no reference can show.** The impact. The payoff. The physics.
   Everything else is already in an image. Economy is what a word is DOING, never a word
   count: cut to a budget and you cut the wrong thing. The approved keeper is 722 words; a
   rejected prompt is 716.
