## 2. THE REPEATED KILLERS, RANKED BY HOW OFTEN THEY RECUR

Each is a real, dated rejection. These are the failure modes the writer must be built against.

1. **Static motion / one even pace.** *"no real movement, then the flower disappears"* ·
   *"5.7 of 9 seconds are stillness"* · *"everything plays at one even middle pace — the
   fast/slow contrast is missing"* · v4: *"lost the pace"*.
2. **Abstract geometry instead of physical cause.** The v4 kill, above. Degrees, screen
   direction, spatial bookkeeping — all of it reads as nothing.
3. **Checklist-shaped action.** *"dive/clip-branch/tumble/land compressed into one stacked
   sentence"* — the beat stops committing to real travel.
4. **Drift from the signed keyframe.** *"extreme from-behind close-up with invented floating
   hearts/particle trails, not the staged wide 2-shot."*
5. **Body-into-object physics.** Fuzzby *"going fully INTO the flower body-first instead of
   face-only with body/wings/legs staying outside."*
6. **Dead delivery.** *"stale emotions and dead delivery needs fuzzbys character his energy."*
7. **Audio that doesn't land.** *"'Nailed it.' came through as an almost-inaudible ~0.4s
   blip"* · *"the chant does not audibly land."*
8. **One impossible state ruins a keeper.** *"airborne + still wings"* — otherwise perfect.
9. **Contradictions between source fields reach the prompt.** *"internalCuts was left stale
   … while other fields said clean flight — a real contradiction feeding the compiled
   prompt."*

## 4. THE SPINE, AS EARNED

Everything below cites a real verdict. Nothing is reasoned ahead of footage.

1. **A timed spine, told in physical prose.** Timecoded structure won the density test; prose
   telling won the final pair. Use both.
2. **Physical cause with visible consequences — never geometry.** The v4 kill.
3. **Speed is shown, never asserted:** *"speed shown only through wing blur, camera, and
   bending stems."*
4. **Contrast of pace is the point.** One even middle pace is a rejection every time.
5. **Never a checklist of verbs.** One cause, consequences chained off it.
6. **Objects are physical and persist.** Faces enter flowers; bodies do not. Nothing vanishes.
7. **Both characters continuously present and acting** — Zenny idle, or appearing late, is a
   rejection. *"actively hovering and collecting pollen the whole time."*
8. **Never an impossible state.** Airborne means wings beating. Grounded means feet planted.
9. **One variable per re-fire.** This is what converged SH1 in eleven takes; every attempt to
   change several things at once lost ground.
