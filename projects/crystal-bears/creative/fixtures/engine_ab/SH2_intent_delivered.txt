Fuzzby is the larger bee and Zenny the smaller, and that difference in size holds in every frame they share. Fuzzby is the one who flies, crashes and collects the pollen. Zenny is the one who stays on her flower and watches.

Shot 1: Bee-height 35mm, one continuous character-led camera move. Begin exactly on @图1.

Zenny stays on the centre of one identifiable pink flower for the whole shot, calm and small, her wings ticking over just enough to hold her there. Whenever her flower moves, she travels with it. She is in frame from first to last.

Fuzzby launches off his own flower fast and gleeful, his joy driving the speed, and drives across the corridor toward a tall golden bloom. His showmanship is what physically betrays him: he holds a delighted grin far too long, playing it out to Zenny instead of watching where he is going, and that held grin is the comedy hinge before one sharp impact. The camera goes with him at his own height rather than following behind.

His speed shows only in what he disturbs. Loose pollen lifts off every head he passes, stems bow and snap upright behind him, and one low petal slaps against his shoulder and spins away. He does not correct for any of it.

He arrives at the golden bloom faster than he meant to and goes in face-first. Only his face enters the flower. His body, wings, arms and legs stay outside it and stay readable the whole time, his back arched and his legs trailing behind him in open air.

The flower head compresses deeply around his face and drives down on its own stem, the whole stem bowing under his weight until it is bent almost double. Loose golden pollen bursts out around the rim of the bloom in a single soft explosion and hangs in the air.

The bent stem loads like a spring and throws him back out. He comes away face-first with his eyes shut, tumbling backwards through his own pollen cloud, and the flower snaps upright behind him and rocks itself still.

The recoil slows him as he drifts back, and the discovery stays playful and alive rather than stunned. He settles into a hover a wingspan from Zenny, blinking, and across his upper lip sit two thick curls of bright pollen-yellow dust that have gathered into a handlebar shape. It is loose granular golden pollen only. Nothing dark anywhere on his face, no fur, no shadow, no chin.

Fuzzby freezes. He crosses his eyes downward, finds the yellow curls sitting on his own lip, and goes completely still for a moment while it lands on him. Then he decides the crash has stamped him with something ridiculous and official, and treats it as an achievement. The preen is its own beat: he is delighted, he sincerely believes it looks official. His chest comes up, his shoulders square, and he strokes one side of the moustache flat with a single proud sweep of one arm.

During the opening spoken section of @Audio1, Fuzzby holds that pose and performs the line to Zenny with his whole body — chest out, chin lifted, the arm still resting against the moustache like a man presenting an award to himself. Zenny watches him with her mouth closed and does not move.

As the final spoken section of @Audio1 begins, Zenny delivers her line flat, without lifting her voice and without moving anything but her mouth. She lets it land. Fuzzby beams back at her, entirely convinced the pollen has promoted him.

Then Zenny turns half away toward her own flower, and only once her face is turning out of Fuzzby's view does one cheek lift into the smallest private smile. She does not let him see it.

Hold on Zenny's half-turned face and that escaping smile for two seconds after the audio finishes. Fuzzby stays hovering beside her, wings still beating, giving the golden moustache one more satisfied stroke. Silence.
