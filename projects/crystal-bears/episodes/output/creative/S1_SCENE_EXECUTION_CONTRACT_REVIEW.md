## Scene Promise

The audience is pulled into Fuzzby's joyful, self-important pollen chase and laughs because his
body and the flower field keep disproving his claims of mastery. Zenny's precise stillness first
makes the chaos warmer and funnier, then becomes quiet care when thunder changes the meaning of
the corridor. The scene leaves on one image containing both truths: Fuzzby is still lovable and
ridiculous, and the storm is real.

## Character Performance Law

- **Fuzzby:** committed authority, never generic clumsiness. He always treats the latest failure
  as proof of expertise. Chest, chin and antennae rebuild status before his body has settled.
- **Zenny:** economy, precision and affectionate truth. Her reaction follows the reveal; she
  never competes with Fuzzby's movement. Her restrained eye movement and delayed smile make the
  deadpan loving rather than cold.
- Preserve the complete approved turnaround proportions at equal depth. Do not inflate Fuzzby's
  body or shrink Zenny. Neither character carries pollen bags, sacks or invented equipment.

## World And Camera Law

- A forgiving bee-height flower corridor with playable depth ahead of both bees.
- The camera follows Fuzzby slightly late but never loses physical cause and effect.
- Flowers and leaves are comic participants: petals compress, stems bow, leaves store and return
  force, and pollen blooms from contact rather than appearing as an opaque effect.
- Camera changes are motivated by changed comic ownership: movement belongs to Fuzzby; the
  judgement and weather recognition belong to Zenny.
- Preserve warm stylised feature-quality 3D CGI, tactile fur, separated pollen particles, soft
  plant materials and readable silhouettes. Use an original Crystal Bears visual language.

## Eight-Beat Execution Spine

### S1.V1 - Busy competence versus chaotic speed

**Canonical owner:** `1.B1`
**Unit/time:** S1.A, 0.0-4.2s
**Script truth:** Zenny collects pollen with smooth precision while Fuzzby zig-zags and performs
his busy chant.
**Directorial intent:** Establish the relationship through movement before any crash: Zenny is
actually competent; Fuzzby wants speed to look like competence.
**Visible proof:** Both bees remain readable at equal depth. Zenny completes one clean flower
collection path. Fuzzby crosses and recrosses the corridor with at least two escalating near
misses, chest leading and altitude porpoising, while his approved opening audio plays. He does
not hit the leaf or complete the first crash during this beat.
**Camera duty:** Travel behind and beside them at bee height, following slightly late while
keeping open corridor ahead.
**End state:** Fuzzby commits too low and too fast toward the pollen flower; Zenny remains on her
clean line.
**Blocking failures:** opening as a portrait; hiding Zenny; crashing before the chase reads;
adding bags or equipment; changing relative scale.

### S1.V2 - Leaf recoil, gymnastic recovery and false triumph

**Canonical owner:** `1.B1`
**Unit/time:** S1.A, 4.2-8.8s
**Script truth:** Fuzzby overdoes the pollen scoop, spins sideways into a leaf, rebounds and says
`Nailed it.`
**Directorial intent:** The audience must see failure before he performs success.
**Visible proof:** The flower contact redirects Fuzzby's sideways momentum into a broad springy
leaf. His weight bends and visibly loads it. The leaf snaps back and launches him into one clear
tucked sideways rotation; he extends out of the rotation into an over-deliberate chest-out hover.
His body still wobbles when the second Fuzzby cue in `@Audio1` lands at 7.2-8.0s. Hold the proud
pose long enough to read the bent leaf recovering and pollen drifting behind him.
**Camera duty:** Keep flower, body-to-leaf contact, leaf compression, recoil and recovery in a
single readable geography; settle for the proud pose.
**End state:** Fuzzby is upright and chest-forward but physically unsettled; Zenny glides close
enough to witness him.
**Blocking failures:** vague or off-screen collision; rigid trampoline leaf; random tumble;
dialogue during impact; pose too brief to read.

### S1.V3 - Pollen moustache reveal and invitation to admire

**Canonical owner:** `1.B2`
**Unit/time:** S1.A, 8.8-13.6s
**Script truth:** Fuzzby puts his whole face into another flower, emerges with a fuzzy yellow
pollen moustache and asks whether he looks official.
**Directorial intent:** He turns evidence of another accident into a badge of rank.
**Visible proof:** Fuzzby relaunches from the wobbling pose, enters one clearly identified flower
face-first, pauses with body and kicking legs outside, then pulls free. Contact with the stamens
visibly transfers two soft yellow pollen curls across his upper lip; no dark hair, beard or chin
mark appears. He discovers Zenny and presents the moustache chest-forward while his question
plays at 11.8-13.0s.
**Camera duty:** Let the flower contact create the moustache on screen, then hold a clean
two-character composition where the mark is unmistakable.
**End state:** Fuzzby proudly offers the pollen moustache for judgement; Zenny is suppressing a
laugh without enlarging her body performance.
**Blocking failures:** moustache appearing without contact; pollen covering the whole face;
cutting before the silhouette reads; Zenny laughing broadly or reacting before the reveal.

### S1.V4 - Zenny's affectionate deadpan re-title

**Canonical owner:** `1.B2`

**Camera duty:** Lock the relationship composition. Do not cut away from either the evidence or

### S1.V5 - The wipe becomes a bolt and a larger confession

**Canonical owner:** `1.B3`
**Unit/time:** S1.B, 0.0-5.8s
**Inherited state:** Begin from Unit A's approved final frame: same positions, light, camera side
and pollen moustache.
**Script truth:** Fuzzby gasps, wipes his face, smears the pollen worse and dives again.
**Directorial intent:** A tiny status repair escalates because he applies the same excessive
force that created the problem.
**Visible proof:** Fuzzby's eyes register Zenny's tease; he attacks the moustache with one fast
paw wipe that visibly spreads the yellow mark across cheek and forehead. He sees the worsening
smear, refuses the embarrassment, throws his chest forward and bolts down the corridor before
his body has settled. The camera preserves the smear as the cause of the relaunch.
**Camera duty:** Start close enough to read wipe-to-smear, then release into a wider chase only
after the worsening mark is visible.
**End state:** Fuzzby is committed to an over-fast blind line toward a branch; Zenny remains the
fixed witness behind him.
**Blocking failures:** cleaning the face; hiding the smear; an unmotivated new chase; random
obstacles before the cause reads; resetting the moustache between units.

### S1.V6 - Chain reaction, Buzz Crash and loving eye-roll

**Canonical owner:** `1.B3`
**Unit/time:** S1.B, 5.8-12.8s
**Script truth:** Fuzzby clips a branch, tumbles through three readable contacts, lands upside
down in a blossom, kicks, pops up for `Buzz Crash!!`, dives again and emerges more pollen-covered
while Zenny rolls her eyes and smiles.
**Directorial intent:** Every recovery attempt becomes physical evidence against him, but Zenny's
ending reaction confirms affection rather than contempt.
**Visible proof:** The smear-driven bolt meets one branch in view. The branch redirects him into
three distinct `THUP` contacts: petals compress, stems bow and leaves recoil in causal order.
He finishes head-first in a blossom with body outside and legs kicking. After a readable still
beat he braces, pops vertically clear and rebuilds an on-parade pose as the Fuzzby cue at
7.5-8.3s lands. Zenny waits until the pop-up has read, then gives one small eye-roll that softens
into a smile. Fuzzby immediately dives once more and reappears visibly more coated in pollen.
**Camera duty:** Follow the causal path rather than cutting randomly; settle first on the kicking
legs and pop-up, then allow Zenny's late reaction to share the frame.
**End state:** More-pollen-covered Fuzzby hovers inside unsettled golden dust; Zenny's small smile
anchors him as the plants continue swaying.
**Blocking failures:** random obstacle montage; hidden branch cause; flower swallowing his whole
body; reaction before the pop-up; missing `Buzz Crash` pose; cold eye-roll; no final worsening.

### S1.V7 - Thunder changes the meaning of the corridor

**Canonical owner:** `1.B4`
**Unit/time:** S1.B, 12.8-20.6s
**Script truth:** Thunder sounds. Fuzzby pauses and comments on the drama; Zenny sees the changed
light and says a storm is coming.
**Directorial intent:** Let the playful field inhale. Fuzzby tries to keep performing; Zenny
becomes the careful character who names reality.
**Visible proof:** Pollen and plant movement settle enough for a distant rumble to create a real
gap. Fuzzby arrests mid-hover with one delayed wobble; his comment plays at 16.1-17.8s. Zenny's
body becomes even quieter. Her gaze moves to the sky as the warm light subtly loses intensity,
then her warning plays at 19.2-20.6s. Neither sees a storm object yet.
**Camera duty:** Begin in the shared aftermath, then let attention move from Fuzzby's wobble to
Zenny's skyward recognition without inflating the warning.
**End state:** Zenny is still and sky-aware; Fuzzby's hover has lost carefree certainty; the
flower corridor remains recognisable but less safe.
**Blocking failures:** thunder treated as another impact gag; immediate dark storm; alarmed
Zenny; unrelated cutaway; sunny reset that erases the tonal change.

### S1.V8 - Pressure boast, immediate capture and warm exit

**Canonical owner:** `1.B5`
**Unit/time:** S1.B, 20.6-26.8s
**Script truth:** Fuzzby claims to work well under pressure, immediately flies into a flower and
gets stuck again; Zenny sighs.
**Directorial intent:** Finish with competence performed and incompetence revealed while keeping
the storm concern alive.
**Visible proof:** Fuzzby converts the warning into a renewed chest-out demonstration. His line
plays at 21.9-24.0s. On its final thought he accelerates along one clean, readable path and plugs
straight into the receiving flower. Petals compress softly around his head and shoulders, his
body and legs remain outside, and pollen trembles rather than exploding. Zenny waits for the
capture to read, then releases one small sigh and a loving not-again eye movement. No further
business follows.
**Camera duty:** Frame the boast, complete flight path and flower capture as one contradiction;
hold the final two-character image with the changed sky-feel present.
**End state / Unit B handoff:** Fuzzby is softly held in the flower, physically contradicted but
safe; Zenny remains nearby with tired affection; the field carries both laughter and storm unease.
**Blocking failures:** impact before the boast reads; painful collision; full-body disappearance;
extra escape gag; cold or angry Zenny; restoring purely sunny light; ending before the sigh.
