# S1.SH1 Split Execution Plan

Status: production workflow rule for the next Scene 1 refire.

## Why This Exists

The pipeline works mechanically. The failure is creative translation under too much density in one provider pass: chase, crash, gymnastic recovery, flower moustache, Zenny judgement, second failure, final crash and warm ending are too many precision comedy events for one 29-second Seedance render.

Use the full 30 seconds where the scene has one clear job. Split when the scene has dense physical comedy, exact reveal geography, route-sensitive causality or competing camera jobs.

## Canonical Shot

Master shot: `S1.SH1`

Goal: Fuzzby's joyful performance of expertise becomes increasingly visible failure, while Zenny's restrained amusement shows she sees and loves exactly who he is.

All units must preserve:

- `@Audio1` as the only dialogue, voice performance, mouth timing and silence source.
- Zenny turnaround as complete identity, proportions, glasses, wings and scale.
- Fuzzby turnaround as complete identity, proportions, glasses, wings and scale.
- Flower corridor plate for layout, materials, warm light and bee-height scale.
- No pollen bags or invented equipment.
- Fuzzby never bulky; preserve turnaround scale at equal depth.
- Zenny stays out of Fuzzby's collision path.

## Production Units

### S1.SH1A - Chase And False Triumph

Target duration: about 8-10 seconds.

Job: establish pace and physical confidence before the first failure.

Camera: late-chasing drone feel at bee height, fast but readable, corridor rushes around Fuzzby.

Beat coverage:

- Fuzzby barrels through flower corridor.
- Two escalating near-misses before any crash.
- He dives too hard into a flower, scoops pollen, spins sideways into a springy leaf.
- Leaf recoil flips him into a tucked rotation and chest-out wobbling hover.
- "Nailed it" lands on false-triumph pose from approved audio.

End handoff frame: Fuzzby hovers upright, chest-forward, still wobbling, pollen drifting. Hold about 1 second.

### S1.SH1B - Flower Moustache And Zenny Judgement

Target duration: about 7-9 seconds.

Job: make the moustache causality unmistakable and let Zenny's judgement land.

Camera: steadier relationship composition; the flower must be visible before, during and after the transfer.

Beat coverage:

- Begin from the approved handoff frame or a matching held keyframe.
- No moustache exists at the start.
- Fuzzby puts his face into a flower. His body remains outside the flower.
- Flower stamens visibly transfer two yellow upper-lip curls.
- Fuzzby pulls back and proudly presents the accident for judgement.
- Zenny arrives into judgement position; her eyes read the moustache before her response.
- Fuzzby absorbs "officially nuts" as if it is a title.

End handoff frame: Fuzzby holds the flower-caused pollen moustache; Zenny is close, amused, not hit. Hold about 1 second.

### S1.SH1C - Failed Correction And Warm Ending

Target duration: about 11-13 seconds.

Job: second failure escalates from the moustache into the final warm confession.

Camera: follow the correction attempt, then settle into a clean two-character final tableau.

Beat coverage:

- Begin with the approved moustache handoff frame.
- Fuzzby wipes at his face and smears the pollen worse.
- He overcommits into another flight path; do not make this a collision with Zenny.
- He clips a branch or flower stem, tumbles through readable flower contacts, lands upside down in a blossom, kicks, pops upright.
- He emerges more pollen-covered than before.
- Zenny eye-rolls only after the final pose reads; the eye-roll softens into affection.

End frame: Fuzzby more pollen-covered than before, golden dust between him and Zenny, Zenny smiling through the eye-roll.

## Timing Rule

Durations are story budgets, not frame-accurate commands. Do not force too many exact timestamp instructions. Exactness belongs to dialogue ownership, reference binding, start/end frame continuity and review.

## Audio Rule

For split units:

- Seedance uses `@Audio1` exactly for dialogue, performance, mouth timing and silence.
- Seedance generates foley and natural ambience only.
- No musical underscore is baked into split renders.
- After approved units are stitched, create or select one ElevenLabs scene-level music cue and duck it under dialogue across the whole scene.

For simple single-unit scenes:

- A native Seedance music test is allowed only when deliberately approved for that whole scene.

## Review Gate

Each unit must be reviewed beat-by-beat before moving forward:

1. References visible and correct.
2. Start state matches approved handoff.
3. Required beat happened in the right order.
4. Cause and payoff are visible.
5. Scale and identity stayed locked to turnarounds.
6. End handoff frame is usable for the next unit.
7. No extra dialogue, subtitles, character drift or accidental collision changes.

Only approved unit renders go into rough cut.
