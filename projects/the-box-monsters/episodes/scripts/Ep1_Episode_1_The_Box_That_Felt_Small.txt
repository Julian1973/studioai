THE BOX MONSTERS
Episode 1: The Box That Felt Small

Runtime: 7 minutes
Structure: 5 scenes / 14 shots / 30 seconds per shot
Emotion: Shame
Production Rule: Each scene has one clean plate and one wide character keyframe. Shots inside the scene carry forward using last frame to first frame.

SCENE 01: CLASSROOM MISTAKE

Runtime: 1 minute
Shots: 01-02
Clean Plate: Empty classroom. Word BRIDGE on the board.
Character Keyframe: Jenny standing near the front of class, worksheet in hand. Wide frame with room around her.

Shot 01: Reading Goes Wrong

Jenny stands at the front of the classroom holding her worksheet.

The word BRIDGE is written on the board.

The room is quiet.

TEACHER
Take your time, Jenny.

Jenny looks at the word. Her fingers tighten around the paper.

JENNY
The little rabbit crossed the...

She pauses.

JENNY
Briggle.

A beat.

One child lets out a tiny laugh. Another child laughs because the first one did.

Jenny’s cheeks flush.

TEACHER
That’s all right. Try again.

Jenny looks at the word again. It feels much bigger now.

JENNY
Bridge.

But her voice is tiny.

Final Frame: Jenny frozen, embarrassed, holding the worksheet close.

Shot 02: The Feeling Stays

Jenny is back at her desk.

The class has moved on, but Jenny has not.

The worksheet lies in front of her. The word BRIDGE seems to stare back.

Jenny slowly folds the paper smaller and smaller.

A kind classmate leans over.

CLASSMATE
You all right?

Jenny gives a tiny nod.

JENNY
Yeah.

But she is not.

She slips the folded worksheet into her school bag.

The school bell rings.

Final Frame: Jenny holding her bag, the folded worksheet hidden inside.

SCENE 02: JENNY’S BEDROOM

Runtime: 1 minute
Shots: 03-04
Clean Plate: Empty bedroom. Shoebox visible under or beside the bed.
Character Keyframe: Jenny kneeling beside the shoebox. Wide frame, no cropping.

Shot 03: The Feeling Goes Into The Box

Jenny kneels on her bedroom floor.

Her decorated shoebox sits beside her bed. It has stars, hearts and one wonky rainbow drawn on the lid.

Jenny opens it.

Inside are small treasures: a ribbon, a pebble, an old badge, a folded card.

She takes out the worksheet.

She looks at it.

JENNY
It wasn’t even that funny.

She places the folded worksheet inside the box.

For a moment, she keeps her hand on it.

JENNY
I don’t want to think about it.

She closes the lid.

The box gives the smallest cardboard sigh.

Jenny does not notice.

Final Frame: The closed shoebox on the bedroom floor.

Shot 04: The Box Calls

Later. Jenny’s bedroom is quiet.

Jenny is asleep.

The shoebox sits still.

Then the lid lifts by the tiniest amount.

A warm golden line glows through the gap.

Inside, the folded worksheet trembles.

A soft cardboard pulse travels from the box, under the bed, through the floorboards.

The glow moves like a little signal through the dark.

The shoebox lid settles again.

Final Frame: Golden light disappearing through the floor.

SCENE 03: THE FOLD

Runtime: 1 minute
Shots: 05-06
Clean Plate: Empty Fold mission platform, cardboard tunnels, warm lamps, tubes and signs.
Character Keyframe: Patch, Rumble, Tilly and Nib reacting to the call. Wide frame.

Shot 05: The Fold Hears It

Inside The Fold, little lamps flicker.

Patch is balancing three boxes on his head.

Nib sits on top of the highest box.

PATCH
Steady. I am a tower of calm.

The signal tube suddenly HONKS.

Patch collapses into a heap of packing paper.

Nib lands perfectly.

NIB
More of a cupboard of panic.

Tilly hurries to a cardboard meter.

A paper strip prints out.

TILLY
Feeling call. Small box. Bedroom route.

Rumble leans down.

RUMBLE
What feeling?

Tilly reads the paper.

Her face softens.

TILLY
Shame.

The team goes still.

Final Frame: A cardboard route opens behind them.

Shot 06: Into The Route

Patch stands and brushes paper from his head.

PATCH
Right. Soft feet. Soft voices. No big fixing.

Tilly looks at him.

TILLY
You once tried to fix sadness with a trumpet.

PATCH
It was a gentle trumpet.

Nib is already halfway into the route.

NIB
To the briggle!

Patch blinks.

PATCH
The what?

Rumble squeezes carefully into the cardboard tunnel.

Tilly grabs her tool roll.

Patch follows.

They rush through folded cardboard, tape bridges, paper flaps and tiny glowing signs.

Final Frame: The route opens into a strange cardboard classroom.

SCENE 04: SHOEBOX SHAME WORLD

Runtime: 3 minutes
Shots: 07-12
Clean Plate: Empty cardboard classroom inside the shoebox. Huge word BRIGGLE on the wall.
Character Keyframe: Patch, Rumble, Tilly and Nib entering the shame world. Wide frame. Jenny’s emotional echo is visible at a distance.

Shot 07: Arrival In The Shoebox World

The Box Monsters tumble into a cardboard classroom.

Everything is handmade from folded paper, old tape and bits of schoolwork.

On the wall, huge letters loom:

BRIGGLE

The word echoes around the room.

BRIGGLE ECHO
Briggle. Briggle. Briggle.

Patch looks up.

PATCH
That word has got very confident.

Rumble covers one ear.

RUMBLE
It is a loud mistake.

Tilly studies the room.

TILLY
This is the moment. The feeling has built itself around it.

Nib pokes a cardboard desk. It squeaks.

NIB
Even the furniture is embarrassed.

They all look ahead.

Jenny’s emotional echo stands near the board, small and curled inward.

Final Frame: The monsters see Jenny’s emotional echo.

Shot 08: Jenny’s Echo

Jenny’s emotional echo holds the worksheet.

She is smaller than real Jenny, almost Patch’s height.

Patch approaches slowly.

PATCH
Hello. We are not here to laugh.

Jenny’s echo looks down.

Patch sits on the floor a little distance away.

PATCH
I have said spoon wrong three times today.

Nib raises a hand.

NIB
He said sploon.

Patch nods.

PATCH
Beautiful word. Wrong, but beautiful.

Jenny’s echo almost looks up.

Rumble sits too, very carefully.

RUMBLE
You do not have to be big yet.

Jenny’s hands loosen slightly.

Final Frame: Jenny’s echo looking at Patch.

Shot 09: The Wrong Fix

Tilly opens her tool roll.

TILLY
Good news. I have identified the issue.

She unfolds a cardboard machine.

A sign reads:

CORRECT ANSWER MACHINE

Patch looks uncertain.

PATCH
That sounds... very answer-y.

Tilly turns the handle.

The machine clunks and spits cards everywhere.

MACHINE
Correct. Correct. Correct. Correct.

Jenny’s echo shrinks.

The word BRIGGLE grows larger.

Rumble notices.

RUMBLE
She is getting smaller.

Tilly stops turning.

TILLY
That was not the intended outcome.

Patch looks at the machine.

PATCH
Maybe correct is not the same as better.

Final Frame: Jenny’s echo smaller, the room darker.

Shot 10: The Walls Press In

The cardboard walls begin to fold inward.

The huge word BRIGGLE repeats louder.

BRIGGLE ECHO
Briggle. Briggle. Briggle.

Rumble presses both hands against one wall.

RUMBLE
I have got this.

The wall pushes harder.

Rumble’s feet slide.

RUMBLE
I have got some of this.

Patch kneels near Jenny’s echo.

PATCH
When I get something wrong, I feel like everyone can see it.

Jenny’s echo looks at him.

Patch places a hand on his chest.

PATCH
But getting it wrong did not make you small.

The room pauses.

The word flickers.

Tilly looks at her machine, understanding.

Final Frame: The walls stop pressing in.

Shot 11: The Briggle Cafe

Nib finds a scrap of cardboard and a pencil.

He writes something, then proudly holds it up.

The sign says:

THE BRIGGLE CAFE

Tilly stares at it.

TILLY
Why?

NIB
Because if I made a mistake that good, I would sell soup from it.

A tiny silence.

Jenny’s echo smiles.

Patch points at the sign.

PATCH
I would visit.

Rumble nods.

RUMBLE
I would order bread.

Tilly tries not to smile.

TILLY
The spelling would need work.

Nib shrugs.

NIB
So would the soup.

Jenny’s echo laughs softly.

The word BRIGGLE shrinks.

Final Frame: Jenny’s echo smiling for the first time.

Shot 12: Try Again

Tilly looks at the Correct Answer Machine.

She tears off the sign.

Then she writes a new one:

TRY AGAIN MACHINE

Patch turns the handle.

The machine clunks gently.

A golden card slides out.

Jenny’s echo picks it up.

It reads:

WRONG IS NOT THE SAME AS SMALL

Jenny’s echo stands taller.

The cardboard classroom warms.

The huge word on the wall changes from BRIGGLE to BRIDGE.

Patch smiles.

PATCH
There it is.

The golden repair light rises from the card and travels upward, out of the shoebox world.

Final Frame: Golden repair light leaving the cardboard classroom.

SCENE 05: MORNING AND PAYOFF

Runtime: 1 minute
Shots: 13-14
Clean Plate: Jenny’s bedroom in morning light. Shoebox visible.
Character Keyframe: Jenny kneeling beside the shoebox, finding the repaired worksheet and golden card. Wide frame.

Shot 13: Morning Repair

Morning light fills Jenny’s bedroom.

Jenny wakes and looks toward the shoebox.

She kneels beside it and opens the lid.

The worksheet is inside, gently repaired with a little strip of golden tape.

Beside it is a small card.

Jenny reads it.

JENNY
Wrong is not the same as small.

She looks confused.

Then she smiles, just a little.

She places the card in her pocket.

She picks up the worksheet.

This time, she does not fold it smaller.

Final Frame: Jenny standing with the worksheet, ready for school.

Shot 14: Bridge Saved

Back in the classroom.

Jenny stands with the worksheet.

The word is there again.

TEACHER
Would you like to try one line?

Jenny breathes in.

She looks nervous, but she stays.

JENNY
The little rabbit crossed the bridge.

A beat.

Then Jenny adds quietly:

JENNY
Not the briggle.

A few children laugh kindly.

Jenny laughs too.

Under her desk, the shoebox gives the smallest warm cardboard twitch.

Far away in The Fold, a little lamp glows.

Nib’s voice echoes faintly.

NIB
Still think the cafe’s a winner.

Jenny smiles.

Cut to warm black.

END
