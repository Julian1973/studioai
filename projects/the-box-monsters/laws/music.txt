Music register (children's cartoon, audience 8-12): the underscore always has ENERGY and forward drive - playful, bright, rhythmic, light percussion and bouncy motifs. Tension is built with momentum (a ticking pulse, a rising line, a held bouncing note), never with slowness: nothing mournful, funereal, dirge-like or ambient-drone. Embarrassment and quiet beats are scored small and light (a plucked hesitation, a cheeky sting), still with pace underneath. Comedy beats get a crisp sting or a cartoon percussion hit.
