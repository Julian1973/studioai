## Director's statement
- **Feel:** Safe laughter first: the meadow feels playful and forgiving before thunder asks for bravery.
- **Whose scene:** Fuzzby drives the comic energy; Zenny owns the emotional temperature and the turn toward danger.
- **Emotional change:** The scene moves from carefree show-off play to a lightly sobered awareness that weather is coming.
- **The laugh:** Fuzzby keeps converting mistakes into heroic branding while Zenny’s restraint makes each boast funnier.
- **Visual surprise:** Flowers, leaves, branches, and pollen behave like spring-loaded comic partners without hiding impact or weight.
- **Carries forward:** Fuzzby is pollen-covered and stuck; Zenny has named the storm, shifting play into concern.
