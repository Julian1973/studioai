FADE IN:


EXT. BUZZING NOOK — MORNING

A golden HONEYCOMB hangs from a branch. KEEN has built a catapult from sticks and a bendy branch. He loads a BERRY. Steps back. One eye closed. Tongue out. He lines up the shot — aiming carefully at the honeycomb. Nearby — ZENNY sits cross-legged on a leaf. Zenny meditates. Keen fires. WHOOSH — the berry flies — SPLAT. Right onto Zenny’s forehead. Juice slowly drips down. Keen ducks for cover and hides. Zenny’s eye twitches. She opens her eyes. Looks around. Sees — FUZZBY asleep on the honeycomb. Fuzzby snores, then awakens, laughing at Zenny. Zenny calmly licks the berry juice from her face. Closes her eyes again.


ZENNY
OK, Fuzzby, calm down. It’s not that funny.


Above — Fuzzby is laughing so much that Keen sees an opportunity.


KEEN
Uh-oh—


He rushes forward — SNATCHES the honeycomb — Fuzzby drops. BUZZ-CRASH. Keen freezes. Beat. The catapult — somehow reset — SNAPS back — THWACK.


KEEN
Ow!


It smacks his tail. The honeycomb flies — bounces on a rock. Rolls across a log — stops in front of Fuzzby. Fuzzby lifts his head. Sees the honey. Looks at Keen.


FUZZBY
Nice machine.


KEEN
It almost worked.


Behind Keen — the catapult collapses. CLATTER. Keen sighs. Walks away. Zenny calmly wipes her face. Settles again. Zenny meditates.


EXT. MOONLIT POOL — MORNING

The forest is still. Morning light falls soft and golden through the trees. A pool sits quietly between the trees. AIDA sits beside it. Still. Peaceful. Eyes closed. Her ROSE QUARTZ PENDANT glows softly. The pool is perfectly still. In the water — an image appears. SUNNY. Arranging her perfect party. Garlands. Cups. That enormous smile. Aida watches the pool. Then — A single raindrop falls. Hits the surface right in the centre of Sunny’s reflection. The image ripples and breaks apart. Aida opens her eyes. Looks up at the sky. Dark clouds gathering. A long beat. Then back at the water.


AIDA
Someone’s day might be a little dampened.


She rises quietly. Brushes herself down. Says nothing to anyone.


EXT. GREAT CLEARING — LATER

Bright sunlight beams down on a pristine outdoor party setup. Everything looks perfect for a lovely afternoon. SUNNY happily straightens a row of berry cups on the table. She adjusts a colorful garland, putting the final touches on the decor just right. She steps back with a satisfied smile.


SUNNY
Cups straight, gorgeous garland… and the weather is perfect. Everything is exactly where it needs to be. Nothing is going to ruin this party.


Plink. A single dark spot appears on a paper napkin.


SUNNY
A little sprinkle is good luck, right? Another drop hits a lantern.


SUNNY
It’s fine! It’s okay! Everything is fine! Then the sky opens up. Rain pours over the decorations. Garlands droop. Lanterns hang wet. Berry cups fill with rainwater. SUNNY rushes around trying to fix everything.


SUNNY
Not good luck! It’s not fine! Save the berry cups! A lantern flickers. PSSST. Out.


Sunny droops. Her shoulders sink. Beat. She suddenly brightens —


SUNNY
New plan! We move the party inside!


INT. PARTY CAVE — CONTINUOUS

Sunny bursts inside, completely soaked, her arms overflowing with soggy, tangled decorations. The other bears immediately jump into action. HOWEY grabs the wet lanterns. MISTY tries to untangle a massive knot of ribbons. LUNA attempts to dry off the flooded berry cups.


SUNNY
Lanterns over here! Ribbons over there! Hurry, before the pretty paper gets muddy!


Amie walks over, holding out a warm, dry towel.


AMIE
Sunny, it’s okay. We are safe inside now. Let’s just dry off.


Sunny shakes her head fast, her wet ears flopping.


SUNNY
No time for drying! We must make the inside look exactly like the outside! Keen, bring the ladder!


Keen gives a little sigh and brings the wooden ladder over to the wall.


KEEN
We don’t need a perfect party, Sunny. We just need our friends.


SUNNY
But I want it to be perfect! Well, I’ll make my own sunshine in here! Just watch!


Sunny climbs up the ladder holding a big, soaking wet string of yellow flowers. She grunts and pushes the wet garland onto the cave wall. It goes SQUISH. It is heavy and sad like a wet sponge. DRIP. DRIP. DRIP. Cold rainwater falls from the wet garland… right onto Keen’s head at the bottom of the ladder. Keen blinks. The water runs right down his nose. Sunny freezes, staring down at him. She gasps loudly, her eyes going wide.


KEEN
Oh… no sunshine today? Just a drop? Instead of calming down, Sunny throws her hands in the air.


SUNNY
Oh no! I’m making it rain inside! The party is getting wetter, not better! She lets go of the wet garland. It falls to the cave floor with a giant SPLAT! Muddy water splashes all over Keen’s toes.


MISTY
Sunny, wait, it’s just a little water —


Keen looks down and wiggles his toes.


KEEN
Actually… don’t worry! I love to splash in muddy puddles!


Keen gives a happy little STOMP. SPLASH!


SUNNY
Muddy puddles?! At a perfect party?! No, no, no!


Sunny zooms past Keen, leaving a trail of wet footprints behind her. Sunny quickly hooks a lantern string to a rock. The knot slips loose. The lantern swings and bumps a stool. The stool tips into HOWEY! Howey bumps MISTY! Misty spins — Her paw flips a honey pot — PLOOP. It lands upside down on FUZZBY like a sticky yellow helmet.


FUZZBY
Oof! I can’t see! He waddles around angrily, waving blindly. ZENNY watches Fuzzby stuck in the honey pot. A beat. She tries to stay composed. Her face tightens — but a small laugh slips out.


FUZZBY
I have been HONEYED!


Sunny rushes over, grabbing Fuzzby and the honey pot. She pulls hard. Fuzzby pulls back. Sunny STRUGGLES. They step side to side, then begin to spin. Sunny, realizing everyone is watching, forces a huge, panicked smile. She tries to pretend it’s all part of the plan.


SUNNY
Grab your partner, do-si-do! Round and round the honey goes! Swing your bear, don’t drop the beat, This indoor party is super sweet!


Howey and Misty look confused but start clapping along anyway. Sunny and Fuzzby spin faster. And faster! Their feet slip on the wet cave floor. Totally out of control.


SUNNY
Whoa, whoa, whoaaaa! They slide across the cave floor and SMASH into a pile of cushions. BUZZ-CRASH.


Sunny pops up out of the cushions, smiling way too hard.


SUNNY
Ta-da! Everyone is having fun… Right?


Fuzzby sits up, dazed and confused. The honey pot is finally off his head, but he is completely covered in golden, gooey honey.


FUZZBY
I’m too sticky for this!


Fuzzby collapses backwards into the cushions with a squish. Beat. Silence. The other bears just stare. Sunny’s big fake smile slowly fades away. She looks around at the wet, muddy, sticky mess. Her glowing pendant flickers, dimming like her mood.


SUNNY
Oh… look at your faces. You guys aren’t having a good time at all, are you?


EXT. CAVE ENTRANCE — MOMENTS LATER

Rain falls softly. Sunny sits on a log holding a soggy acorn crown. She shakes it. Splat.


SUNNY
All I wanted was the perfect party for my friends.


Beat.


SUNNY
I wanted everyone to feel bright and sunny but now I feel empty.


She looks down at the muddy ground. Rain taps the leaves. LUNA steps out of the cave. She walks over and gently sits beside her on the wet log.


LUNA
You are SUNNY… even in the rain.


SUNNY
I wanted a sunny day for a perfect party BUT it wasn’t perfect.


LUNA
Nothing can ever be perfectly perfect. And that is okay.


Luna reaches out and gently takes the soggy acorn crown from Sunny’s paws.


LUNA
When things go wrong, we just must take a little time to appreciate what DOES go right.


Sunny looks up at her friend, wiping a raindrop from her cheek.


LUNA
We have a cozy cave. We have sweet berries to eat. And we have all our best friends together. There is always a way to look on the bright side… even if it’s raining.


Luna looks into the distance and sees a bright purple crystal seem to appear. She isn’t sure whether she is seeing things.


LUNA
Did you see that?


SUNNY
See what?


LUNA
Don’t worry.


Sunny looks back towards the warm glow of the cave entrance. She takes a long, deep breath. A tiny, real smile finally appears on her face. Her pendant gives a soft, warm glow. Sunny leans gently against Luna. SUNNY touches her crystal.


SUNNY
Bright as the sun, ready for fun — Citrine, let happiness run.


Luna touches her crystal.


LUNA
With quiet and might, I trust my sight — Lepidolite, reveal what’s right! Lavender light spreads. Golden light joins. Rain becomes shimmering prisms. HOWEY steps out. A rainbow lands on his nose. He spins trying to catch it.


Howey and Misty join outside. Nobody minds the rain. Howey places a soggy garland on Sunny’s head like a crown. It flops over one eye. Sunny giggles.


SUNNY
That is the worst crown ever.


HOWEY
I know.


Beat.


SUNNY
I love it. Suddenly — SPLASH! KEEN, followed by AMIE, bursts out of the cave, running right past them and jumping into the middle of a giant muddy puddle.


KEEN
Muddy puddles! Best party ever!


Keen grabs Howey’s paws. They start stomping and spinning in the mud. Keen calls out, singing at the top of his lungs.


KEEN
Grab your partner, do-si-do! Round and round the puddle goes! Swing your bear, don’t drop the beat, This rainy party is super sweet!


Howey giggles, stomping his feet to the rhythm. SPLASH! SPLASH! Sunny watches them spin. Her smile grows bigger and bigger, lighting up her whole face. She jumps off the log, wearing the soggy acorn crown.


SUNNY
Wait for me!


Sunny runs right into the rain, grabbing Luna’s paw along the way. They jump into the giant muddy puddle with Howey and Keen. SPLOOSH! The bears laugh, stomp, and square dance in the rain. Sunny’s pendant glows bright and warm. It isn’t a sunny day, but the party is finally perfect. Across the clearing, AIDA watches quietly. In the background, Fuzzby flies straight into the purple crystal that Luna spotted. Dazed, he says to himself:


FUZZBY
Didn’t see that before.


EXT. BUZZING NOOK — LATE AFTERNOON

Rain puddles fill the forest floor. The prized honeycomb rests on a log. FUZZBY stands at the top of a muddy slope. He shakes his soaked wings. Useless. He eyes the honeycomb.


FUZZBY
This time… I glide. He belly-slides down the mud on his little bee tummy. WHOOOOSH. He zips right past the honeycomb. Off the log. SPLASH. He face-plants into a puddle.


He stands on his six little legs — slips — SPLASH. Again. Keen strolls up. He looks down at the tiny, muddy bee. Smirking, Keen offers a giant paw. Fuzzby reaches out his tiny bee arms, grabs hold of Keen’s paw, and YANKS with all his tiny might. Keen loses his balance.


KEEN
Whoa! They both slip — SPLASH! They slide together right beneath the log, totally covered in mud. The honeycomb rests directly above them. A golden honey drop forms on the comb and falls. PLOP. Right between Fuzzby’s little antennae, down onto his nose.


Fuzzby crosses his eyes to look at it, then zips his little tongue out and licks it.


FUZZBY
Worth it. Beat. Another drop forms and falls. PLOP. Right onto Keen’s snout.


Keen wipes it off with a giant paw and licks it.


KEEN
Definitely worth it. Beat. Keen and Fuzzby both look straight up at the honeycomb. They open their mouths as wide as they go. They start elbowing each other out of the way to catch the next drop.


END.
