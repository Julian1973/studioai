FADE IN:
EXT. BUZZING NOOK – MORNING	1

Golden morning light. Dew sparkles on wildflowers.
A honeycomb sits on a small stump. KEEN tiptoes toward it.
He lifts his knees high with every step.

CRUNCH.
CRUNCH.
CRUNCH.

Keen freezes each time. He spots the honeycomb. His eyes widen. He LICKS HIS LIPS. He reaches toward the honey.
FUZZBY the bee is asleep on top of the honeycomb.
FUZZBY
(snoring)
	ZZZZZ …

A small snore bubble grows from his nose.
Keen reaches closer to the honey.
POP! The bubble bursts.
Keen FREEZES.
FUZZBY
(snoring,again)
	ZZZZZ …

Another bubble slowly grows.
Keen inches closer to the honey.

POP! The second bubble bursts.

A tiny puff of yellow pollen floats through the air…
… and lands right on Keen's nose.

Beat.

Keen's nose TWITCHES. His eyes water. He tries to hold it.
He holds it. He holds it. He almost stops —

KEEN
(Sneeze)
	ACHOO!

Fuzzby's eyes POP open.
Beat.
KEEN
(sheepishly and recovering from the sneeze and stammering)
	ACHOO! … Oh, Ah, Hi Fuzzby
		
			FUZZBY
	Keen – No matter how hard	you try, you are never, ever, ever, ever, ever going to get my honeycomb. 

Fuzzby's eyes close again. Instant SNORING.
KEEN
Never … Ever?
FUZZBY
(snoring,again)
	…ever … ZZZZZ … never zzzz … bye keen

Keen walks away from the honeycomb, amused rather than defeated.
KEEN
(under his breath, joking)
	We’ll see, Fuzzby. We’ll see.

Nearby, ZENNY sits cross-legged on a leaf, eyes closed. As Keen recedes along the woodland path in the background, Zenny opens one eye, smiles, gives a small wink, then closes her eye again.
ZENNY
(calm, warm, sustained)
	oooohhhhhhhhmmmmmmmmmmmm

CUT TO:
EXT. CRYSTAL CLEARING – MORNING	2
Soft mist floats through tall trees. AIDA sits beside a crystal bowl. She slowly circles a mallet around the rim. A warm tone hums through the clearing. Her ROSE QUARTZ PENDANT begins to glow. Pink light fills her eyes.

VISION – THROUGH AIDA'S EYES

A small red squirrel sits inside a hollow tree. He packs a satchel. Unpacks it. Packs it again. He doesn't move.
BACK TO SCENE.
Aida gently stops the bowl. She places a paw on her heart.
AIDA
(softly)
Someone needs a little help today. (AIDA reacts to off camera SNEEZE)
KEEN
AHHHHCHHOOOOO!!! AHHHHCHHOOOOO!!!

Across the clearing Keen brushes pollen from his nose.
AIDA
Bless you. Sounds like someone has been sniffing around Fuzzby’s honeycomb. Keen? Come with me

Keen still recovering from his sneezing attack jogs over.
KEEN
(sniffles)
Coming, Aida!
	AHHHHCHHOOOOO!!!
AIDA
(softly)
Bless you Keen.

CUT TO:
INT. BO'S HOLLOW OAK – DAY	3
Warm sunlight fills a cozy hollow tree. BO, a young red squirrel, sits beside a small satchel. Next to him lies a shiny CONKER. His Mum calls to him from offscreen, we never actually see his Mum.
BO’S MUM
BO, it’s time for you to go to the Learning Circle? 
Don’t forget your lunch.

Bo calls back to his Mum
BO
I’ll go in a minute Mum.

(to the conker) Or forty. 
Forty sounds much better to me.

He places the conker in the bag and walks towards the front door but just as he opens the door Bo freezes in place. 
Panic sets in.

BO
(to himself)
Maybe, I’ll go tomorrow instead. 
Tomorrow sounds much better than today.
Tomorrow everything will OK, not today. 

Bo scurries back inside and takes the Conker out of his bag. Bo realizes he must go – today. 

BO
Today could be OK. 

He sighs deeply and then puts the Conker back in the bag again. But his anxiety starts to rise again.

BO

But, Today might not be OK. 

Bo pulls the Conker out of the bag -again - and holds it in his hands. Panic really kicks in. 

BO
(realizing)
What if it’s not OK?!

A shadow appears at the doorway.
Keen appears at the open door and gently taps on the hollow tree. Shave and a Haircut knock. He peeks inside.

Beat.
KEEN
Hi! I'm Keen.
	

Bo jumps. The conker flies into the air. He catches it, fumbles it several times and finally hugs it. BO looks from the Conker to KEEN immediately 
KEEN
Aida sent me.
She thought we should meet.
BO
(nervous)
		Hi Keen, my name is Bo and I am really worried. What if nobody likes me at the Learning Circle? I don't think I can go today. What if my tail does the THING?

His tail suddenly POOFS twice its size. Bo grabs it.
BO
	Stop it! Please just be a normal tail! 

The tail refuses. POOFS twice its size again.

Keen stares. Impressed.
KEEN
Whoa.
	Your tail is AMAZING. 

Bo pauses his struggle with his tail and begins to calm down.
BO
…you think so?
KEEN
Mine does nothing.
	Watch.

Keen turns around and wiggles. His tail hangs there. Completely still.
KEEN
See?
	Nothing.

Bo looks at Keen's tail. Then at his own. His tail slowly deflates. He almost smiles.

						Keen
				Let’s go for a walk and get some 
fresh air. Fresh air always helps.

BO’s smile grows to a full smile.

						
BO
				That does sound nice.

His tail deflates, picks up his bag and walks out the door with Keen.

CUT TO:

EXT. FOREST PATH – DAY	4
A winding path through the Whispering Woods. Sunlight flickers through the leaves. Bo walks beside Keen.
Step. Step. BO Walks with a newfound confidence, no longer concerned about his tail doing 'THE THING'. Keen and Bo walk along doing a countdown, each time they get to 1, BO’s tail does 'THE THING' on command. The tails POOFS UP and then shrinks back to normal before POOFING UP again each time they count.

29	KEEN
3, 2, 1…

Bo’s tail does THE THING on command. POOF!

30	BO & KEEN
3, 2, 1…

BO SPINS and twirls. POOF! The tail does THE THING again.

31	BO
3, 2, 1…

POOF! Bo giggles. They continue walking. Bo lightly kicks leaves as he moves. BO hears the bears chatter from the Learning Circle nearby. Bo suddenly stops. He grips his satchel tightly. His smile fades.

32	BO
(quiet)
I’m STILL worried about going to the Learning Circle.
He slowly sits on a nearby fallen log. Keen gently sits on a fallen log beside him. He gives Bo space.

33	BO
They all know each other.

Beat.
34	BO
But I don’t know them, and they don’t know me. 

KEEN
It can be hard to meet new people, but it’s good to make new friends.

Bo stares down the path toward the Learning Circle. AIDA walks down the path towards BO and Keen.

35	AIDA
Hi Keen, I see you have met a new friend.

Bo glances up, curious. 

36	KEEN
Yes.
Keen smiles and places his paw around BO’s shoulder.
					
					KEEN
Bo, this is Aida.

Aida sits beside Bo, leaving space between them. She notices his satchel.

37	AIDA
You packed your bag.

38	BO
Four times.

39	AIDA
(warm)
I would have done five.

Bo looks at her. He softens.

40	BO
You get scared too?

41	AIDA
Every single time.

42	BO
But you still go?

43	AIDA
Every single time.

Beat.

Wind moves gently through the trees.

CUT TO:

EXT. FOREST PATH – CONTINUOUS	5
Aida offers her paw. Bo hesitates. Keen gives a small nod. Aida touches her pendant.

44	AIDA
With heart open wide, I stand with pride —
Rose Quartz, be our guide!

Pink light spreads across the forest floor. Leaves gently lift and shimmer in the glow. Keen touches his wristbands.

45	KEEN
Brave and bright, with all my might —
Aquamarine, shine with light!

Blue light joins the pink. The forest path glows.
Bo is amazed and smiles bigger than ever before as the light surrounds him and reaches his feet and continues down the path towards the Learning Circle. He looks down. Bo takes a breath. He steps forward. Then another step. They walk together.
46	BO
(confidently to himself)
Every single time.
CUT TO:

EXT. LEARNING CIRCLE – DAY	6
Luna, Sunny, Keen, Howie, Misty, Aida, and Aime sit together in a sunny clearing. BO joins the circle but stands. SUNNY jumps up excitedly, practically bouncing AND DANCING.

47	SUNNY
HI! I’m Sunny! Do you like cake? I like singing and dancing… and cake! I mean who doesn’t like cake?!?

Sunny spins happily in a quick little twirl.

48	LUNA
Sunny. Breathe.

Sunny takes a huge breath.

49	SUNNY
(takes a deep relaxing breath)
Right. Breathing.

Bo carefully sits on a cushion. His tail suddenly POOFS huge. Everyone sees. Bo freezes — unsure of how the group will react.
Beat.

50	MISTY
(delighted)
Can it do that again?

The tail POOFS again. Bigger.
51	AMIE
Do it again!

52	BO
3, 2, 1…

POOF! Bo’s tail does THE THING to the group’s delight.
Aime giggles Bo laughs.

53	Aime
(giggles)
It’s so fluffy!
The groups giggles together enjoying the company of their new friends. Bo is happy and confident as a part of the group giggling.

CUT TO:
EXT. LEARNING CIRCLE – LATER	7
Afternoon sunlight fills the clearing. Bo draws pictures of the bears on a large leaf. He adjusts the leaf slightly as he draws. Keen sits beside him, watching.
54	BO
That one is you. Look at your tail.

Keen lifts the drawing. His tail in the picture is enormous.
55	KEEN
My tail is doing a THING!

They laugh. Bo shifts the drawing slightly.
56	BO
(quiet)
I’m glad I came to the Learning Circle – even though I was scared to at first.
57	KEEN
Me too.
Bo’s tail suddenly POOFS twice its size.  Bo and Keen giggle. Aida watches from the edge of the clearing. Her pendant glows softly. She smiles.

CUT TO:
EXT. KEEN’S GARDEN – EVENING	8
Golden sunset light. Keens honeycomb still sits untouched as he tends to the flowers watering his garden.

Amongst the flowers FUZZBY is wearing a FLOWER DISGUISE — petals around his head like he’s trying to blend in. He smiles — pretending to be a flower.

As Keens approaches a tiny puff of pollen floats through the air … landing right on Keen’s nose. Keen’s nose TWITCHES. He tries to hold it — shaking slightly—
	KEEN
(Sneeze)
ACHOO!
His sneeze blows Fuzzbys petals and costume completely off exposing Fuzzby. He peeks around grinning sheepishly at KEEN. 
58	KEEN
Never, ever – Fuzzby!

59	FUZZBY
Ever… Bzzzzz… Ever?



WIDE SHOT — Peaceful Garden. Honeycomb untouched.

FADE OUT.

END.
