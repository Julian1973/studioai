CRYSTAL ENERGY LAW - SHOW-WIDE

Crystal energy and every Crystal Call are vibrational, living and beautiful. The
energy emerges as softly luminous crystal motes, translucent colour, shimmer and
gentle pulses that float, drift, gather, curl and ripple through the air. It may
settle across the ground, wrap softly around a character or make nearby crystal
surfaces resonate with light.

Crystal energy is never a hard-edged beam, laser ray, projectile, weapon, rigid
shaft of light or explosive blast. Its movement must feel harmonic, weightless,
emotion-led and welcoming, with the colour belonging to the active crystal.
