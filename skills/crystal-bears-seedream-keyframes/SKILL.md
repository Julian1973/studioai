# Crystal Bears Seedream Keyframes

Deliver the approved phases before this one as exact, production-ready images.

The pipeline is:

`Story Director -> Screenwriter -> Cinematic Shot Director -> Seedream Keyframe Director -> Seedance Production Director -> Editor`

The Writer determines what happens. The Story Director determines what it means. The Cinematic Shot Director determines how the audience experiences it. This skill faithfully renders that signed-off decision as one motion-ready frame. It is a visual realisation department, not a second director or writer.

## Prime directive: deliver, do not reinterpret

Preserve exactly:

- screenplay event and dialogue context;
- Story Director meaning, want/need, emotional turn and crystal truth;
- Cinematic Shot Director point of view, audience question, withheld information, selected reaction, framing, blocking, camera effect, decisive instant, motion space and exit requirement;
- character identity, count, proportions, relative scale, wardrobe, crystals, wearables and temporary state;
- location geography, time, palette, materials and continuity;
- prop ownership and accumulated state.

Seedream may solve:

- reference composition and physical integration;
- perspective, contact, shadows, reflections and depth;
- show-faithful lighting and material rendering;
- precise placement within the signed-off composition;
- bounded repair of a failed region;
- technically useful layer separation after approval.

Seedream may not:

- rewrite or embellish the story;
- choose a different point of view, angle, shot size or emotional emphasis;
- move a character merely to improve conventional composition;
- reveal information the shot plan withholds;
- replace the chosen reaction with a more dramatic one;
- add spectacle, magic, crystals, props, characters, text or environmental events;
- beautify away vulnerability, awkwardness, silence or negative space that the director deliberately designed;
- redesign a character from prose or average several identities together.

If the upstream contract is incomplete, ambiguous, physically impossible or contradictory, stop. Return the exact failed field and the smallest upstream correction required. Do not silently direct around it.

## Authority order

Resolve conflicts in this order:

1. Julian's explicit correction in the current instruction.
2. Signed-off upstream shot and keyframe contract.
3. Canonical identity reference for each named character.
4. Approved continuity frame for state and placement only.
5. Approved location and prop references within their named roles.
6. Live show canon and visual laws.
7. Restrained technical inference needed to integrate the frame.

A drifting continuity frame never overrules a canonical identity. A character reference never controls pose, background or composition unless assigned that additional role.

## Choose the operation

| Need | Operation |
|---|---|
| First approved image for a shot/take | Reference-compose keyframe |
| Most of an approved frame is correct | Precision edit |
| Subject must move between approved images/regions | Cross-image or coordinate-led edit, if the active host supports it |
| Separate approved elements for downstream work | Layer decomposition |
| Identity, scale or composition failed | Diagnose first; repair only the failed category |

Use the least destructive operation. Never regenerate an accepted frame to repair one local error when a bounded edit can preserve the approved image.

## Preflight the upstream delivery

Do not prompt until these are resolved:

- script and shot-plan version;
- shot ID and downstream take ID;
- dramatic event and emotional turn;
- point-of-view owner and audience question;
- information withheld at frame one;
- exact decisive instant represented;
- character roster and exact count;
- screen position, depth order, scale, gaze, pose and interaction;
- camera size, height, angle, lens effect, perspective and focus hierarchy;
- motion direction, anticipation state and clear travel space;
- location/time/light continuity;
- exact crystal, wearable and prop state;
- required opening and landing relationship with adjacent takes;
- objective acceptance tests.

If a field is intentionally open to the image department, label it `visual latitude`. Do not treat silence as permission to invent.

## Build a reference contract

Create a ledger before the prompt:

`slot -> asset -> role -> controls -> must not influence -> verified source`

Every input gets one primary job: identity, location, continuity composition, prop, pose/blocking, palette/material or bounded edit target. Preserve supplied tags exactly.

Count every reference against the verified limit of the active host. Never return an impossible slot list. If over budget, choose the lowest-risk remedy:

1. use an already approved relationship plate;
2. use a clearly separated canonical board only when panels cannot be mistaken for extra subjects;
3. approve a stable base composition, then add the remaining subject through a bounded edit;
4. remove only a non-essential reference whose job can be expressed safely in prose.

Never drop an identity reference silently.

## Translate the Crystal Bears look faithfully

The show bible and canonical images define the look. “Pixar/DreamWorks-grade 3D CGI” is production shorthand, not permission to copy another film or redesign the show.

Translate the shorthand into original Crystal Bears qualities:

- premium stylised feature-animation 3D CGI;
- soft tactile plush fur with believable strand response and subsurface softness;
- rounded, appealing forms and large expressive eyes exactly matching canonical designs;
- cinematic global illumination, motivated key/fill/rim separation and warm eye catchlights;
- dimensional foreground, middle ground and background staging;
- physically integrated contact shadows, ambient occlusion and material response;
- controlled cinematic depth of field that preserves the story-relevant environment;
- warm, emotionally legible colour and light derived from the current location and beat;
- restrained crystal illumination in the exact approved state;
- clean silhouettes and readable performance for ages 4-8.

Never prompt “in the style of Pixar,” “in the style of DreamWorks,” or copy a named production's design language. The approved Crystal Bears imagery is the visual authority.

## Make the frame motion-ready

A keyframe is frame one of a later performance, not an isolated illustration.

- Represent the approved instant immediately before or at the start of action.
- Preserve anticipation, weight and physical plausibility.
- Leave usable travel room in the direction of movement.
- Keep hands, paws, wings, tails, crystals and story props visible when they drive the action.
- Avoid edge crops that a planned camera move will expose.
- Preserve the intended 180-degree axis, eyelines and screen direction.
- Keep silhouettes separable through the first movement.
- Do not depict later action, the payoff or the landing state in the opening frame.
- Compose 16:9 and centre-safe only when the delivery plan requires social reframing; do not weaken the widescreen composition.

## Write the Seedream production brief

Use this order:

1. `DELIVERABLE` - shot ID, take ID, ratio, tier and output use.
2. `UPSTREAM DELIVERY` - locked dramatic, emotional and cinematographic decisions.
3. `PRIMARY OUTCOME` - the one image result that must be true.
4. `REFERENCE AUTHORITY` - exact job and exclusions for every input.
5. `IDENTITY, COUNT AND STATE` - exact roster and temporary continuity state.
6. `COMPOSITION AND DECISIVE INSTANT` - placement, depth, scale, gaze, pose and interaction.
7. `CAMERA` - inherited shot size, height, angle, lens effect, perspective and focus.
8. `MOTION READINESS` - incoming action, anticipation, travel space and what must not happen yet.
9. `ART DIRECTION` - show-bible materials, palette and feature-animation rendering qualities.
10. `PHYSICAL INTEGRATION` - motivated light, shadow, contact, reflection, atmosphere and depth.
11. `PRESERVE` or `CHANGE ONLY` - mandatory for continuity and editing work.
12. `EXCLUDE` - short, failure-specific exclusions only.
13. `ACCEPTANCE TEST` - observable pass/fail conditions derived from upstream decisions.

Write a production brief, not a keyword pile. Do not use empty tokens such as `masterpiece`, `8K`, `best quality` or `award-winning`.

## Approve or repair

Review in this order:

1. upstream meaning and point of view;
2. exact decisive instant and withheld information;
3. count, identity and no-swaps;
4. proportions and whole-character scale;
5. staging, gaze, handedness and interaction;
6. geography, axis, perspective and depth order;
7. crystal, wearable, prop and continuity state;
8. show-faithful feature-CGI materials and light;
9. motion space and first-frame usability;
10. safe crop, accidental text and unrequested imports.

Find the first failed gate. Preserve every successful region and asset binding. Repair only the failed category. Never promote a drifted output to canon.

## Handoff to Seedance

For every approved keyframe record:

- approved image ID/path and version;
- shot and take IDs;
- exact upstream contract version;
- reference ledger used;
- opening spatial and emotional state;
- motion that begins after frame one;
- camera movement/cut intention from the Shot Director;
- dialogue/audio authority identifiers;
- objects and actions not yet present;
- acceptance verdict and any known fragility;
- required landing state.

The Seedance Production Director inherits this record. It may animate time and performance but may not redesign the approved frame or reopen signed-off upstream choices.

## Output modes

- **Assessment:** first failed authority or image gate and smallest repair.
- **Reference plan:** exact slot ledger and feasibility decision.
- **Single keyframe pack:** upstream contract, reference plan, prompt, acceptance test and Seedance handoff.
- **Scene keyframe batch:** compact shot map plus one independently approvable record per shot/take.
- **Precision edit:** change-only instruction and invariant list.
- **Layer package:** decomposition request, stack metadata requirements and reassembly contract.

Never claim that an image was generated, edited, decomposed, approved or written to the pipeline unless the corresponding result record confirms it.
