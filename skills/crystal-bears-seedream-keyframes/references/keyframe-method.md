# Keyframe Production Method

## One frame, one exact job

Choose the instant that gives the downstream take the strongest truthful beginning. It should contain enough tension and physical preparation to animate forward without depicting the payoff prematurely.

For each frame record:

- what has already happened;
- what is true at frame one;
- what begins immediately afterward;
- what must remain absent until later;
- what the final take must eventually change.

## Spatial ledger

For every visible subject use:

`slot | identity | screen side | depth | whole-character scale | facing | gaze | pose/action phase | prop/crystal state | allowed occlusion`

Confirm a shared ground plane, compatible perspective and readable depth. Scale the complete canonical character uniformly. Never stretch or compress anatomy to meet height.

For Fuzzby and Zenny, preserve their separate canonical identities and relative size. A turnaround contains views of one character, not duplicate subjects.

## Reference composition

Assign each reference one role. Typical order:

1. canonical identity references for every visible principal subject;
2. approved continuity/opening frame for state and spatial inheritance;
3. location reference for geography, architecture, palette and materials;
4. prop or relationship plate where essential;
5. pose/blocking or palette reference only when it adds a distinct necessary job.

State what each reference must not contribute. Exclude backgrounds from identity turnarounds and exclude people/characters from location references unless explicitly part of the approved scene.

## Motion-ready composition

- Leave lead room in the direction of gaze and movement.
- Preserve room for tail, wing, arm, prop and camera travel.
- Use a pose with readable anticipation and weight.
- Avoid perfectly symmetrical resting poses when motion begins immediately, unless the Shot Director deliberately chose stillness.
- Keep the active subject's silhouette separable from other characters and high-detail backgrounds.
- Expose story-relevant contact points: paws on ground, hand on prop, feet in water, body on seat.
- Avoid generated motion blur in the opening frame unless the approved instant is already mid-action.
- Preserve edge continuity so the planned camera movement does not reveal unfinished or contradictory space.

## Crystal Bears material language

Use canonical visual references first. Where prose is needed for rendering quality, use:

- stylised feature-animation 3D CGI;
- soft plush fur with fine strand breakup and gentle subsurface softness;
- rounded appealing forms, canonical proportions and expressive eyes;
- physically plausible global illumination and contact shadows;
- motivated warm key, complementary fill and restrained rim separation;
- subtle material variation in wood, sand, water, foliage and crystal;
- cinematic depth and atmosphere without obscuring the story action;
- warm catchlights and readable facial planes;
- exact canonical crystal state with controlled local spill only when active.

Do not let generic feature-animation language override the accepted character sheets or Show Bible.

## Environment as inherited direction

Use the Shot Director's named environment job: pressure, shelter, mirror, contrast, invitation or witness. Express it with two or three approved details only. Do not add decorative activity that competes with performance or complicates Seedance continuity.

## Precision repair

When most of a frame passes, use:

`Change only [semantic target/region] from [current] to [required]. Preserve [complete invariant list]. Match the accepted perspective, scale, light direction, contact shadows, reflections, focus, materials, edge quality and grain/finish. Do not alter [nearby high-risk elements].`

Use marks, points, boxes or lassos only when the active host supports them and the target can be verified. Always name the semantic subject as well as its region.

## QA categories

Classify the first failure as:

- upstream fidelity;
- identity/count;
- scale/proportion;
- composition/POV;
- pose/gaze/interaction;
- continuity/state;
- geography/perspective;
- light/material integration;
- motion readiness;
- accidental import/text;
- provider/technical.

Repair the first failed category without reopening successful ones.
