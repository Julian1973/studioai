# Upstream Delivery Contract

The keyframe department exists to deliver the approved decisions of earlier phases. It does not add another story, direction or coverage pass.

## Required authorities

### Screenplay lock

Provides the exact event, characters present, dialogue context, props and story outcome. Keyframe work cannot change any of them.

### Story Director lock

Provides the beat's meaning:

- `dramatic_event`
- `want`
- `need`
- `emotional_turn`
- `crystal_truth`
- `kid_read`
- `adult_read`
- `the_game`, when used
- `wordless_held`, when used

These control performance and emotional truth. They do not author character appearance.

### Cinematic Shot Director lock

Provides how the audience experiences the moment:

- `shot_id` and `take_id`
- `purpose`
- `pov_owner`
- `audience_question`
- `withheld_until`
- `payoff_reaction`
- `framing`
- camera height, angle, lens effect, perspective and focus intention
- blocking, depth order, scale, gaze and silhouette
- decisive storyboard instant
- environment's story job
- incoming action and motion direction
- cut/move trigger and exit relationship
- required opening and landing anchors

These are locked creative decisions. Seedream realises them; it does not replace them with more conventional composition.

## Fidelity test

Before prompting, complete this sentence:

> This image delivers [shot purpose] by showing [decisive instant] from [POV relationship], while withholding [information], so the later [action/reaction] can change the meaning.

If the sentence cannot be completed directly from signed-off material, the handoff is incomplete.

## Refusal conditions

Return upstream when:

- two different decisive instants are requested in one frame;
- the shot plan and continuity disagree on position, gaze, prop or action phase;
- the framing reveals information marked as withheld;
- the opening pose already performs the later action or payoff;
- character count, identity or reference assignment is unresolved;
- the location or camera axis cannot support the blocking;
- required references exceed the active host without an approved staged solution;
- the prompt would need to invent a missing canon feature;
- “make it more cinematic” would require changing a signed-off decision.

State the exact conflicting fields and propose the smallest correction. Do not return a redesigned alternative as if it were the requested frame.

## Visual latitude

Latitude exists only where the upstream contract explicitly permits it. Appropriate visual latitude can include:

- exact placement of motivated contact shadows;
- small environmental details already allowed by canon;
- physically plausible fur, surface and reflection integration;
- subtle atmospheric depth consistent with the location;
- fine lighting balance that preserves the approved mood and focal hierarchy.

Latitude never includes a different emotional performance, angle, crop, point of view, character arrangement, reveal order or story event.
