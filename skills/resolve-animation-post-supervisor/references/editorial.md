# Editorial and continuity

## Source and scene intent
Bind the candidate, approved screenplay version, canon, exact voice authority, original take IDs and intended opening/closing states. A generated alternative is not approved because it exists. Use the Director's source hierarchy to resolve authority; surface genuine conflicts. Retain historical exports and reversible working timelines.

Choose original-shot assembly, flattened-source reconstruction or hybrid conform. For a flattened movie, shot detection is a candidate map: verify boundaries manually in motion, including internal cuts within a single generated clip. Recover exact approved original frames when available and prove alignment before replacing a region. Do not invent missing handles, clean plates or coverage.

## Cut inspection
Before polishing joins, watch the scene in context. Identify what the audience needs to understand, the protagonist's change and the beat that makes the ending earned. A cut may reveal information, thought, relationship, action, anticipation or release; repetition and a held reaction may be the point. Do not remove a repeated image merely because it resembles another shot. Do not manufacture coverage or rewrite an approved sequence to satisfy an editorial formula.

Build around the designated performance authority. When genuine dialogue stems exist, an audio-only pass can expose intention, breaths, hesitation, interruption and comic timing. With a flattened soundtrack, use full-mix playback and acknowledge that isolated dialogue is unavailable. Protect synchronization and meaningful reactions when choosing picture cuts. Expectation, delay, surprise, reaction and payoff are useful comedy questions, not a compulsory five-shot pattern.

Assess readability for the project's actual audience: who wants what, who is speaking, where the action happens, what causes the change and whether the payoff has time to register. An agent's assessment is not a claim that children have watched or understood the film.

At each junction inspect a short moving window spanning both sides, then individual outgoing/incoming frames as needed. Record source and timeline frames with explicit frame-rate/timecode basis. Inspect:
- position, facing, gaze, scale, axis and depth order;
- action phase, velocity, foot contact, pose and camera motion;
- prop/wardrobe state, identities, light and background geography;
- spoken-word boundaries, breaths, ambience and music phrasing;
- what new information or performance the cut contributes.

A changed composition can be intentional; compare against dramatic intent. A repeated beat may serve comedy. Avoid automatic rejection by shot length or similarity.

Repair by cause, not a fixed ladder: trim a corrupt head/tail if usable handles remain; select approved compatible coverage for a motivated reaction or insert; restore clean original frames; use a restrained reframe when it preserves spatial truth. A J/L cut improves audio flow but cannot create missing visual action. Never blanket-dissolve jump cuts or interpolate across a change of identity/pose to hide the fault.

For absent action, issue a targeted Director handoff: approved source IDs, last clean frame/state, first required incoming state, action delta, screen direction, required duration from measured audio, locked dialogue/audio, allowable camera change, ending handoff and failed gate. Generation itself follows the existing Studio approval/provider route.

## Fusion
Use a duplicate/version for small tracked patches, stable plate extensions, masks, titles and genuinely usable holds. Check occlusion, motion blur, edges and temporal consistency across the entire affected span. Do not manufacture speech with frozen/parallax faces or rewrite story information through a patch. Stop a test that fails identity or motion consistency; preserve the successful original elements and escalate the precise defect.

## Defect routing
### Continuation intake record
Derive this from existing records and inspected media when a continued-shot join needs work. Unknown fields stay unknown; do not require the director to complete another form. Use explicit units and declare whether frame ranges are inclusive or exclusive.

```text
Project / episode / scene / join ID:
Outgoing source hash, source in/out and intended edit-out:
Incoming source hash, source in/out and intended edit-in:
Resolve project / timeline ID / version:
Frame rate / timecode basis / range convention:
Inspection mode and reviewed ranges: full motion / sampled / unverified
Verified picture and audio handles (each side):

Outgoing state:
  character positions, facing and scale:
  gaze, eyelines and screen direction:
  action phase, velocity and contact:
  camera, framing and motion:
  props, environment and lighting:
  dialogue, breath, ambience and music phrase:
Incoming requirement:
  first required readable action:
  allowed camera change:
  protected dialogue/audio:
  intended handoff and final stable state:

Decision: edit / repair / regenerate / redesign / preserve intentional contrast
Reason and source evidence:
Resulting candidate hash and affected timeline ranges:
Verification state / remaining limitation:
```

- **EDIT:** existing approved coverage or genuine handles support a clearer cut without losing required action, dialogue or meaning.
- **REPAIR:** a bounded image, colour or sound correction can remain credible throughout the affected moving span.
- **REGENERATE:** a source performance, identity or essential action fails and no verified approved alternative resolves it. Supply the precise shot brief through the existing production path.
- **REDESIGN:** the available coverage or intended construction cannot communicate the required beat. Return a proposed coverage change to the director, preserving approved story until a change is authorized.

A defect can be reclassified as evidence improves. Preserve the reason and before/after ranges. Missing originals first require source recovery; repeated unsuccessful cosmetic fixes do not substitute for a structural decision. Verify geography and synchronized sound again after inserting a reaction, cutaway or reverse angle; a motivated cut need not preserve identical framing or lens.

## Readback
After every timing edit, verify clip starts/durations/source ranges, affected track enable states, sync and downstream scene timing. On this bridge, end-frame assumptions have previously failed: measure the inserted result rather than prescribing duration-minus-one universally. Read back after errors before retrying mutations to avoid duplicate clips/tracks.
