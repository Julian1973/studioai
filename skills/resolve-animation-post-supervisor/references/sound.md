# Sound and lip-sync

Read the Director's audio-lock reference. Approved HEAR owns voice and performance; generated guide audio is not presumed sample-identical. Never silently regenerate, voice-convert, remove words or time-stretch an approved performance.

## Input modes
With genuine stems, use separate dialogue, vocal reaction, foley, effects, ambience and music tracks/buses as useful. Do not duplicate baked programme audio under stems. Name buses clearly (DX, FOLEY, SFX, AMB, MUSIC, MASTER); verify routing and solo/mute results.
With flattened audio, retain the complete original mix as reference. Source separation produces provisional estimates with bleed/artifacts, not authentic stems. Isolated dialogue/music mute tests are unavailable until credible stems exist. Local shifts also move music, effects and ambience; report and audition this cost. Do not prescribe whole-film offsets from one shot.

## Diagnose before repair
Compare export against input for codec priming, start offset, timebase, sample rate, routing, clipping, packet discontinuity and unintended gain. Then compare the matching original shot's moving picture and audio at several anchors near beginning, middle and end. Avoid ambiguous held frames, silence and repetitive music when matching. Record lag convention, units, correlation and uncertainty.

Separate constant offset (candidate local shift), cumulative drift (investigate clock/frame-rate/conform), and mouth animation inconsistent with approved speech (visual/source repair). Correlation only establishes correspondence; it does not validate visible phonemes or prove the original was in sync. Inspect audible consonant/vowel events and mouth closures in real playback at the actual delivery cadence.

For a reversible shift, preserve the mix reference, choose boundaries with valid handles, check no lost/duplicated syllable or onset and audition both joins. Short fades are a testable technique, not proof of click removal. Do not replace an entire mixed region from a source clip without proving music/effects and level continuity. Changing dialogue timing may require picture repair rather than retiming locked HEAR.

## Listening and mix
Listen to the full rendered episode and targeted repaired joins when audio audition is supported. Check clicks, dropouts, repeated/truncated words, pumping, sibilance, breath continuity, intelligibility, score masking, ambience seams and effects sync. Measure integrated loudness, true peak, range and channels against the named delivery specification; no universal target is a substitute for that specification. Use automation to support focus and preserve intentional dynamics.

If the agent cannot hear, state it plainly, mark listening and perceptual sync unverified, prepare timecoded excerpts or a review player and continue objective analysis. Do not transcribe waveform shape, infer glitches from isolated peaks, or treat ASR repetitions as confirmed repeated audio. Numerical checks alone cannot confer sound lock.

## Episode music and scene handoffs
### Sound-handoff decision
1. **Identify the source by region.** Genuine stems: test score automation, tail shaping, delayed entrance or a supported ambience bridge. Flattened programme mix: locate a phrase boundary without protected dialogue, critical foley, action onset or lip-sync dependency. If none exists, retain the source and report the limitation; do not claim a music-only repair. Hybrid: map which tracks are genuine stems and which retain embedded audio on each side; avoid doubling baked dialogue/music underneath stems. Treat separated estimates as provisional, not genuine stems.
2. **Establish intent.** If the transition is deliberately abrupt, audition it in programme context and preserve it if it serves the story without an unacceptable intelligibility or comfort problem. Intentional contrast may justify no edit even when stems exist.
3. **Test only a supported change.** Otherwise choose the smallest reversible handoff that the actual source allows, preserving protected performance and synchronization.
4. **Verify across both sides.** Record the inspected ranges, result and remaining limitation after audition. Without audition, mark repaired-awaiting-check or unverified; a tool response is not a listening result.

Listen for the episode's overall build, breathing space, motifs, contrast and emotional payoff. Record only decision-useful notes in the existing repair ledger: scene/join IDs, source hashes, outgoing/incoming ranges, observed musical phrase and energy, dialogue/effects that must survive, intended handoff, available stems or mixed source, proposed action and verification state. Optional 0–5 energy labels describe perceived intensity; they do not measure LUFS, dictate a climax count or decide approval.

Choose the handoff that serves the actual cut: an outgoing phrase resolving into a new cue; a shared existing ambience or musical bed; deliberate silence; or a deliberate hard contrast. A held reaction, completed action, carried motion or unresolved question can each end a scene. No choice is mandatory, and no fixed decompression duration applies. A forceful exit into a forceful entrance merits contextual listening, not automatic rejection.

Where the user retains Seedance sound, preserve it as the starting authority. With true stems, test music-tail automation, a delayed entrance, or a supported ambience bridge while preserving dialogue and effects. With a mixed track, any fade affects all components; first find a phrase boundary free of protected words and meaningful effects. If a clean change cannot be made, retain the source and explain the precise limitation or propose a separately authorized stem/separation test. Do not create an ambience bed, replacement cue or synthetic tail from nothing and call it preserved source audio.

Audition both sides together at a consistent normal monitoring level, then in the full scene/episode. Check phrases, speech, breaths, action onsets, ambience, level changes and lip-sync. Useful issue labels include music collision, masked dialogue, clipped tail and ambience break; creating a marker is not completing the repair. Measure programme loudness and peaks against the selected delivery specification separately from this creative judgment.
