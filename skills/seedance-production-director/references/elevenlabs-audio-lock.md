# ElevenLabs v3 performance and audio authority

## Before audio exists

The Voice Director prepares performance from the approved words, character voice mapping,
physical action and listener: objective, subtext, tactic, emphasis, meaningful pauses and
restrained supported v3 tags. Preserve exact word order and dialogue occurrence IDs.
Use the Studio's registered voice IDs and supported tag recipes. Never invent voice IDs,
clone a voice or change provider settings. Timing is an estimate until measured.

Return the typed VoiceDirection record and performedText consumed by the existing voice
compiler. Audio generation follows the existing quoted production action. Julian listens
and accepts the actual performance; a good written brief does not constitute audio approval.

## After audio approval

Bind the accepted file, content identity, source dialogue version and measured speaker
intervals. It owns delivery, cadence, pauses and mouth timing for that version of the words.
Only the active speaker articulates; listeners act silently. Preserve intentional nonverbal
material according to the shot's current audio contract.

A changed line requires new voice direction/audio for that line and affected WATCH work.
Retain the old accepted recording as history. It cannot override the corrected screenplay.

## Route-specific compilation

The selected Studio provider contract determines reference tags, audio input support and
whether output retains the exact source waveform or needs the existing editorial restoration.
Never infer native audio-led lip-sync from a product name. Do not introduce a new fallback
or replace the approved route through this skill. The compiler owns the exact audio-authority
clause and transcript placement. Verify actual final audio and visible sync during review.

## Measured timing

Record duration, speaker in/out offsets, exact words, meaningful pauses, silent acting time
and landing reserve from actual media. Keep estimates labelled. Derive every segment from
the same accepted source timeline; do not stretch, paraphrase or regenerate accepted speech
as an invisible repair. A provider's inability to honour an input is a named route issue.
