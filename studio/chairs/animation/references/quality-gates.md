# Prompt quality gates

Score each category internally from 0 to 2. Revise if the total is below 17/20 or if story, canon, dialogue/audio, or continuity scores 0.

| Gate | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story beat | unclear or altered | present but soft | exact, legible change |
| Canon | conflict/invention | incomplete | verified and preserved |
| Physical staging | impossible/summary | partly playable | causal and filmable |
| Camera/edit | contradictory/decorative | serviceable | motivated and clear |
| Performance | generic emotion | some behaviour | intention and micro-reaction |
| Composition | spatially confusing | readable | depth and eyelines support beat |
| Production value | generic adjectives | some specificity | motivated light/material/world detail |
| Dialogue/audio | changed/invented | correct but weakly staged | exact, attributed, playable |
| Continuity | broken/absent | broad handoff | precise opening and closing state |
| Prompt economy | constraint wall/noise | minor excess | every sentence earns its place |

## Final checks

- The clip can be understood without reading the source script.
- The prompt delivers one dramatic unit, not an entire scene squeezed into 15 seconds.
- Every shot has a distinct narrative purpose.
- Lens and movement choices agree.
- Screen direction, eyelines, and character positions are possible.
- Action is sequential enough for the duration.
- Dialogue is verbatim and attributed to the correct speaker.
- No unapproved vocalisation or sound is implied.
- Canon comes from verified sources; creative detail does not redesign the world.
- References have distinct roles and exact tags.
- The final state can open the next clip.
- Safeguards are specific to real failure risks.
- No sentence merely says the result should be good, cinematic, magical, premium, or high quality.

## Mandatory extension gates

For any continuation, these are pass/fail in addition to the score:

- The named master clip is approved and free of the drift being corrected.
- The selected operation is verified or accurately described as conditional.
- Already-completed actions and current audiovisual state are recorded.
- The prompt advances only the new delta.
- The opening preserves motion phase, screen direction, camera axis, light and sound.
- Original canon references—not a drifted frame—control identity and scale.
- The final-frame still does not compete with the master video unless narrowly scoped.
- The landing picture and sound are usable by the next generation.

Fail fast at the first broken extension gate. Do not compensate for a bad master with more prompt text.
