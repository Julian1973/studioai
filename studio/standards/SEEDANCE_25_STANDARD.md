# Seedance 2.5 Production Standard

Status: controlled walk-forward standard, 27 August 2026.

## Authority Labels

- `OFFICIAL`: supported by current primary provider documentation.
- `HOUSE`: adopted Crystal Bears production law backed by our own evidence.
- `PRACTITIONER`: useful external observation awaiting our controlled test.
- `UNVERIFIED`: claim or control that must not reach an executable request.

Provider capability, interface controls and studio practice are separate facts. The
canonical capability record is
`skills/seedance-production-director/references/official-capabilities.md`; do not
duplicate changing limits or interface claims elsewhere.

## Production Laws

1. Script, approved audio, approved masters and signed canon outrank invention.
2. The approved Heart Director contract controls emotional purpose; shot direction
   must make that purpose visible through behavior, staging, image and sound.
3. One reference has one declared role and one authority boundary.
4. A generation carries one playable dramatic unit with no more than three causal
   stages and three motivated views.
5. Thirty seconds is available capacity. Duration follows performance, dialogue,
   physical complexity and reference risk.
6. Dialogue audio controls words, acting, cadence and timing. Spoken words stay out
   of the Crystal Bears provider prompt.
7. Extensions use an approved source clip, an already-true ledger, new delta only,
   literal lighting/audio carry and a designed landing state.
8. Human review is authoritative at Story & Direction, SEE, HEAR and WATCH.

## Promotion Rule

Practitioner advice becomes `HOUSE` only after a bounded test records the prompt,
references, provider route, returned media, observed result and human judgement.
Failed or contradictory advice remains recorded so it cannot be silently re-imported.

This public repository contains no private Seedream customer standard.
